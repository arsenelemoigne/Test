How long Qwen3-0.6B thinks, and when it pays

The untouched model on the data we train on and the questions it is tested on, by kind of task. Length is everything it writes for one answer, thinking included. Each question was asked several times, which shows how much luck moves both the length and the score.

What each dataset looks like

Mean over questions. "Spread" is the typical gap in length between two answers to the same question, as a share of its mean (coefficient of variation).

Q1 · How long are the answers?

Tokens per answer, question by question

How many questions fall at each length (log scale). Bars are scaled within each row; the black tick is the median question. A pile-up at the right edge means answers hitting the token cap.

Q2 · Does thinking longer help?

Accuracy against length

Questions are split into five equal groups by mean length. Each point is a group: its median length and the share of answers that were correct. A falling line means long answers are the ones the model gets wrong.

Q3 · Same question, same answer?

How often repeated answers agree

For every question asked several times: all answers right, all wrong, or a mix. Mixed questions are where luck decides the score.

Q4 · Same question, same length?

Length spread across repeated answers

For each question, the standard deviation of its answer lengths divided by their mean, shown as a histogram per dataset. 0.5 means two answers to the same question typically differ by half their length.
