The goal is more than 55% saved at better than −2 points on questions the model never saw (BottleCap’s own releases sit at 37–46% / −0.7 to −1.0).

Only unseen-question scores are reliable

“Unseen ×8” = 240 random test questions never used for any choice, 8 answers each. The “usual” questions were also used to pick checkpoints, so they flatter by 0.5–5 points.
