=== 3. WHAT MVT IS (short) ===
For each correct reply of the model, we measure after every paragraph (or every 32-token batch) how
sure the model is of its own correct answer (teacher forcing). The MVT says: stop thinking at the
first point where no later part of the thinking pays better per token than the average so far,
g(t)/(t + c). c ("travel cost") is patience: c=1024 stays longer, c=256 stops sooner. The cut trace is
kept only if the model, answering naturally from it twice, is still correct. Then LoRA SFT on the cut
traces, and evaluation.
