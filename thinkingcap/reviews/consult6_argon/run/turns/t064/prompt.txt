# Fact-check of the consult6 (Gemini 4 Argon) report, 11 Nov 2026

Checked against EXPERIMENT_LOG.md (L = line) and all_runs.json by an independent agent; nothing edited.

**Overall:** raw transcription is good (almost every per-run and per-part number matches, including the full-test table and the
720-optimism averages: +1.52 saved / +0.47 accuracy over 9 models). Interpretation is unreliable: ~10 load-bearing causal claims
are wrong, overstated or unsourced. Use it as a correctly indexed scoreboard plus hypotheses; not its win probabilities or its
"proved" statements.

## The five points that matter

1. **Path #3 is built on a competitor's recipe.** D4 (lpd-budget) = bidax5q (bida-exact settings, bida's pairs, "bida settings +
   his math") + DAST's per-question length target (L4131-4136). The report's solve-rate tiers are not in the log. D4 was also not
   the best single pass: v1 = bidax5q 46.7/-0.5 (+1.8 vs curve) beat it (49.0/-1.42, +1.4; 720 only).
2. **rpI evidence for Path #1 is overstated.** rpI did drift longer: 50.4 (rpE-80, 720) -> 42.9 by its step 100 (full test 47.0 ->
   41.9); it held only between steps 100 and 120; single seed. The "+count-rules" IFEval gain did not appear in rpH. The
   counterfactual "rpE-40 + rpI's gains -> 50.6 / -1.13" mixes gains measured at 42% saved into a 50.6%-saved model: not evidence.
   Path #1 stays plausible, but its 55-65% win probability has no measured basis.
3. **"bidax6p proved bf16 loses ~2.8 pp" is wrong.** The log: fp32 "moves the model along the curve rather than a big jump above
   it" (-11 pts saving for +3 pts accuracy; at equal saving +0.3 to +1.1 vs curve; L4662-4668, 4684). Path #2's "fp32 + no-fit
   lifts pt-mid by > 2.2 pp" shrinks to ~0.7 pp (no-fit alone).
4. **Section 1's "already falsified" evidence is padded.** zbench 0.508 is only the position baseline (MLP judge 0.736, own
   confidence 0.717; on SVAMP own confidence 0.87-0.88 and timing AUC 0.96; L1543-1547, 1618-1621). gold1 / teach1c failed from
   an unanchored BCE loss (the model stopped answering), not from DPO on forced cuts. stopV2's lengthening came from long-chosen
   guard pairs + the NLL anchor. "4+4 split fixed benchlot" is wrong (benchlot used 20 forced answers; the 4+4 split is lot V2).
   The verdict against forced-cut DPO still stands on stopB3 (-3.2, code -8.4), stopB5 (-2.7) and stopV3 (stalls at 25%), but the
   "a 0.6B LoRA cannot route budgets" leg is weak.
5. **720 numbers presented as walls or breakthroughs** next to full tests (l2s50e 53.5/-2.81, push2 52.4/-3.00, margin 53.3/-2.70,
   D4, rpB, rpC, lpt6mix are 720 screens; several are bida-pair runs; the "-2.5 to -3.5 wall" ignores bidax5q-final 46.7/-0.59 and
   budget-final 49.0/-1.42). IFEval floor details unsourced or misattributed ("265 response tokens", "-0.65 pp from ir-full",
   "counting rules caused 100% of IFEval's -2.6"; and L5042 says IFEval's loss is NOT a shortened response). The arithmetic itself
   holds: IFEval ~40% and BFCL ~41% saved mean math and code need ~58% for a 50% headline.
