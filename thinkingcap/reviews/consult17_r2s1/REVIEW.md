### 3. Will 1,200 New IFEval Questions $\times 8$ Reach 288 IFEval `shorten` Pairs from Distinct Questions?

**Yes — with $>98\%$ probability under the most conservative rate and $>99.9\%$ under the `ifeval_bida` source distribution.**

Here is the exact math from the stored rollouts in the pack:

1. **How many additional `shorten` candidates are actually needed:**
   - In `dry_run_summary_first_version.txt` (`p1src` alone: `2,206` IFEval questions $\times 10$ rollouts, of which `1,309` [$59.34\%$] match `counting(q[0])` and `897` [$40.66\%$] are non-counting), `repair_pairs.py` found:
     - **`177` clear `shorten` candidates** ($\ge 1.40\times$ thinking ratio) and **`26` backup `shorten_soft` candidates** ($1.20\text{--}1.40\times$ thinking ratio) = **`203` distinct-question `shorten` candidates** (`22.63%` of non-counting questions; `9.20%` of all IFEval questions).

### 4. Settings to Change Before Launch (and Why)

While `r2s1` will run cleanly and build all **2,880** pairs once the file-existence guard (Section 2, Bug 1) is safe, **there is a critical empirical issue with `r2s1`'s expected token saving at step 180 that you should weigh before launch**:

1. **Why `r2s1` as Queued Will Likely Under-Shorten ($\sim 31\%\text{--}36\%$ Saved on Dev-512 vs. the $46.0\%\text{--}51.5\%$ Target):**
   - **Direct Pack Evidence (`p1r1s0` vs. `p1cr1s0`):** We already ran two 1-round DPO experiments from `Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B` on `p1src` with `--lr 2e-5 --lr-min 5e-6 --warmup 10`:
     - **`p1r1s0`** (`3,040` pairs: **$55.9\%$ `shorten`** [`1,700`], **$44.1\%$ `be_right`** [`1,340`], **`0` `guard`**, **`0` `loop`**; overall `chosen_longer = 17.1%`) reached **`47.5%` saved at step 100, `43.7%` at step 150, and `45.9%` at step 200 on Dev-512 (`43.4% / -1.50` on Full Test)**.
     - **`p1cr1s0`** (`3,025` pairs: **$55.2\%$ `shorten`** [`1,670`], **$33.4\%$ `be_right`** [`1,011`], **$6.6\%$ widened `guard`** [`200`, where `chosen` is longer in $92.5\%$ of pairs: median `956 vs 668` thinking tokens], **$4.8\%$ `loop`** [`144`, where `chosen` has median `662` thinking tokens on math and `1,012` on code]; overall `chosen_longer = 18.1%`) reached only **`34.6% / +2.03` at step 100, `32.4% / +2.51` at step 150, and `34.4% / +2.90` at step 200 on Dev-512 (`37.7% -> 28.1% -> 31.7%` on Dev-SVAMP-200)**!
   - **Now compare `r2s1` to `p1cr1s0` (`dry_run_summary_reuse_version.txt`):**
     - `r2s1` has **fewer `shorten` pairs than `p1cr1s0`**: **`1,444 / 2,880 = 50.1%` `shorten`** (math $40\%$ vs. $45\%$, code $60\%$ vs. $70\%$) vs. `p1cr1s0`'s **$55.2\%$ `shorten`** (`1,670 / 3,025`).
     - `r2s1` adds `90` widened `guard` pairs on `mbpp` (median chosen `1,295` vs. rejected `794` thinking tokens) on top of `108` widened `guard` pairs on `svamp` (median chosen `940` vs. rejected `541` thinking tokens), plus `138` `loop` pairs (`36` math, `54` code, `48` IFEval).
     - As a result, the fraction of pairs in `r2s1` where `chosen` is **longer** than `rejected` rises to **`25%` on `svamp`, `18%` on `mbpp` (`13%` in `p1cr1s0`), and `28%` on `ifeval` (`25%` in `p1cr1s0`)**, and median `chosen / rejected` tokens on `mbpp` rise from `568 / 1276` (`0.445`) in `p1cr1s0` to **`694 / 1198` (`0.579`)** in `r2s1`!
     - Why do widened `guard` pairs (`chosen` $\sim 1,000\text{--}1,300$ tokens vs. `rejected` $\sim 540\text{--}790$ tokens) and math/code `loop` pairs (`chosen` $\sim 600\text{--}1,000$ tokens vs. `4096/8192` cap hits) brake compression so strongly when starting from `original`, whereas they worked in `rpM` from `rpE-80`? Because in reference-anchored DPO ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}} = -\log\sigma(\beta[\Delta \log\pi_\theta - \Delta \log\pi_{\text{ref}}])
$), as proved by **SimPO (`arXiv:2405.14734`)** and **olak (`arXiv:2502.04463`)**, the log-probability margin $\Delta \log\pi_\theta$ scales with sequence length. On `original`, a widened `guard` pair with a `1,000`-token chosen trace actively rewards generating $1,000$-token multi-paragraph verifications, canceling out the `shorten` pairs (`341` vs `694` tokens on math; `447` vs `1,175` on code) after step 100 (`p1cr1s0` math thinking grew from `703` tokens at step 100 to `802` at step 150!). Conversely, on `rpE-80` (`rpM`), median right thinking was already compressed to $\sim 280\text{--}450$ tokens, so widened guards held `rpE-80` at $\sim 54\%$ saved instead of $\sim 34\%$.

2. **Recommended Pre-Launch Changes (Prioritized):**
   - **Change A (Critical if you want Step 1 to hit the $\sim 46\%\text{--}51\%$ Dev-512 gate instead of plateauing at $\sim 33\%$ like `p1cr1s0`):** Drop `--guard-wide,svamp+mbpp,--guard-wide-max,0.75` in Step 1 (keep standard `<0.50x` early-stop guards or reserve widened guards for Step 2 / Step 3 where $\tilde{L}_{\text{think}}$ is already compressed, as in `rpM`), and use `--mix-preset round1c` or boost `shorten` shares in `r2s1` to **math $55\%$ `shorten` / $40\%$ `be_right` / $5\%$ `loop`** and **code $70\%$ `shorten` / $22.5\%$ `be_right` / $7.5\%$ `loop`**. *(Pack evidence: `p1r1s0` at $60/40$ math and $70/30$ code with no widened guards achieved `47.5%` Dev-512 saving at step 100 and `45.9%` at step 200; `p1cr1s0` with $15\%$ widened guards dropped to `34.4%`.)*

If you prefer to keep `--no-shorten-counting` and generate the 1,200 `ifeval_bida` questions, keep the command as is (with the `questions.jsonl` guard from Bug 1).
   - **Change C (`--rp-epochs 1` instead of `--rp-epochs 2`):** Since $2,880 / 16 = 180$ steps is exactly $1.00$ epoch, `--rp-epochs 1` guarantees training never wraps around and repeats early pairs if any part ever falls slightly short.
