   6: 
   7: pi and ref are summed log-probabilities of the reply tokens (thinking and answer) under the
   8: policy and under the starting adapter; the reference is scored once before training. The NLL
   9: anchor on the chosen reply (as in RPO, Pang et al. 2024) stops DPO from widening the margin by
  10: lowering both sides. Pair kinds are mixed to --mix (shorten, guard, hard, right), or all kept with --mix all.

  14: nothing changes at test time, where the model is never asked to judge.

  16: Every --save-every optimizer steps a checkpoint is saved. The tripwire watches held-out chosen
  17: replies: if the probability that the reply ends right after </think> rises above --tripwire, a
  18: collapse known from on-policy truncation DPO, training stops and the last checkpoint is kept.
