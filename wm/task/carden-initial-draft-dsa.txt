PREAMBLE
This DATA SHARING AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is entered into as of April 14, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:
Carden Analytics, Inc., a corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of Delaware, with its principal place of business at 1400 Wharton Boulevard, Suite 800, Wilmington, Delaware 19801 (hereinafter referred to as "Carden" or "Data Provider");
and
Luminos Insights Group, LLC, a limited liability company organized and existing under the laws of the State of Maryland, with its principal place of business at 7200 Columbia Gateway Drive, Suite 350, Columbia, Maryland 21046 (hereinafter referred to as "Luminos" or "Data Recipient").
Carden and Luminos are each sometimes referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."
RECITALS.  WHEREAS, Carden aggregates and pseudonymizes point-of-sale consumer transaction data from its merchant network comprising retailers, hospitality providers, and service-industry participants across the United States, and is willing to make certain of that data available to Luminos under the terms and conditions set forth herein (Recital A);
WHEREAS, Luminos is a data-analytics and market-intelligence firm that wishes to receive and process such pseudonymized consumer transaction data for the purpose of creating benchmarking products, analytical indices, and market-trend reports for distribution to its institutional clients, including financial institutions, investment managers, and corporate strategy teams (Recital B);
WHEREAS, the Parties desire to set forth the terms and conditions governing the sharing, use, protection, retention, deletion, and disposition of such data, including the respective rights and obligations of the Parties with respect to confidentiality, security, data use limitations, and related matters (Recital C);
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, promises, and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:
SECTION 1 — DEFINITIONS
As used in this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Section 1 shall have the meanings ascribed to them elsewhere in this Agreement.
1.1 "Agreement" means this Data Sharing Agreement, including all exhibits, schedules, appendices, and amendments hereto, as each may be amended, restated, supplemented, or otherwise modified from time to time in accordance with the terms hereof.
1.2 "Applicable Law" means all federal, state, and local statutes, laws, rules, regulations, ordinances, codes, binding guidance, and regulatory interpretations applicable to the receipt, processing, use, storage, transmission, retention, deletion, or protection of the Shared Data Set, including, without limitation, the Federal Trade Commission Act, the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act), the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act, the Colorado Privacy Act, the Connecticut Data Privacy Act, and any other applicable state comprehensive privacy statute, together with any regulations promulgated thereunder.
1.3 "Authorized Purpose" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.
1.4 "Benchmarking Reports" means the aggregated, non-personal analytical reports, indices, dashboards, and other derivative analytical products produced by Luminos from the Shared Data Set for distribution to Luminos's institutional clients, which reports do not contain record-level data and are sufficiently aggregated such that no individual consumer or individual merchant can be identified therefrom.
1.5 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other Party in connection with this Agreement, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, including the Shared Data Set, the terms and conditions of this Agreement, each Party's proprietary business information, financial data, technical specifications, pricing information, business plans, customer lists, and trade secrets, together with all notes, analyses, compilations, studies, and other materials prepared by the receiving Party to the extent containing or reflecting such information.
1.6 "Data-Access Fee" means the quarterly fee payable by Luminos to Carden in consideration of Carden's provision of the Shared Data Set, as specified in Section 5.1.
1.7 "Effective Date" means the date on which both Parties have duly executed this Agreement, as set forth in the preamble hereto.
1.8 "Discovery" means the point in time at which Luminos, its personnel, or any Subprocessor first reasonably suspects or identifies a Security Incident, irrespective of whether the incident has been confirmed, forensically validated, fully scoped, or subjected to root-cause analysis.
1.9 "Pseudonymized" means processed in such a manner that the data can no longer be attributed to a specific consumer without the use of additional information, where such additional information is kept separately and is subject to appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to ensure that the data is not attributed to an identified or identifiable natural person.
1.10 "Re-Identification" means any attempt, whether direct or indirect, successful or unsuccessful, to reverse pseudonymization, re-link, cross-reference, or otherwise associate any element of the Shared Data Set with an identified or identifiable natural person, including through the use of external data sets, publicly available information, machine-learning inference, probabilistic matching, or any other method or technology.
1.11 "Security Incident" means any unauthorized access to, acquisition of, disclosure of, loss of, alteration of, or use of the Shared Data Set, or any reasonably suspected occurrence thereof, whether resulting from a cyberattack, system compromise, insider threat, physical breach, human error, or any other cause.
1.12 "Shared Data Set" means the pseudonymized consumer point-of-sale transaction records originating from Carden's merchant network that are disclosed to Luminos pursuant to this Agreement, estimated at approximately thirty-eight million (38,000,000) records per calendar quarter, as further described in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference.
1.13 "Subprocessor" means any third-party entity, including contractors, cloud-service providers, managed-service providers, and consultants, engaged by Luminos to store, process, transmit, analyze, or otherwise handle any portion of the Shared Data Set on Luminos's behalf.
1.14 "Term" has the meaning set forth in Section 12.1, and includes the Initial Term and any Renewal Term(s).
1.15 "Transfer Impact Assessment" or "TIA" means Carden's standardized assessment evaluating the legal regime, governmental surveillance practices, enforcement landscape, rule-of-law framework, and technical safeguards applicable to a proposed cross-border transfer of the Shared Data Set to a jurisdiction outside the United States.
1.16 "Vendor-Security Assessment" or "VSA" means Carden's proprietary security-evaluation process administered by Carden's Information Security team, encompassing a comprehensive review of technical, organizational, and contractual controls maintained by the entity under assessment, including but not limited to access controls, encryption standards, incident-response capabilities, personnel-security practices, and business-continuity planning.
SECTION 2 — SCOPE OF DATA SHARING
2.1 Provision of Shared Data Set. Subject to and in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement, Carden agrees to make the Shared Data Set available to Luminos during the Term. Carden shall use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver the Shared Data Set in a timely manner and in accordance with the delivery schedule set forth in Exhibit A; provided, however, that Carden makes no representation or warranty regarding the completeness, accuracy, or fitness for any particular purpose of the Shared Data Set, except as expressly set forth herein.
2.2 Delivery Method and Format. The Shared Data Set will be delivered on a quarterly basis in the file format and via the secure transmission method specified in Exhibit A. Carden reserves the right to modify the delivery method or file format upon reasonable prior written notice to Luminos, provided that any such modification does not materially impair Luminos's ability to ingest and process the Shared Data Set for the Authorized Purpose.
2.3 Pseudonymization. All data within the Shared Data Set has been pseudonymized by Carden prior to delivery to Luminos. Carden retains sole custody and control of all additional information necessary to re-attribute the Shared Data Set to identified or identifiable natural persons, and Carden shall not, at any time, disclose such additional information to Luminos. Luminos acknowledges that the pseudonymization applied by Carden is designed to reduce the risk of consumer identification but does not render the data anonymous, and that the Shared Data Set accordingly remains subject to the protections set forth in this Agreement.
2.4 Ownership. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to transfer ownership of the Shared Data Set, or any intellectual property rights therein, to Luminos. Carden retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Shared Data Set, including all proprietary rights arising under contract, statute, common law, or otherwise. Luminos's rights with respect to the Shared Data Set are limited to the license expressly granted herein for the Authorized Purpose during the Term, and no other rights are granted by implication, estoppel, or otherwise.
SECTION 3 — CONFIDENTIALITY
3.1 Obligation of Confidentiality. Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall use at least the same degree of care to protect such Confidential Information as it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care. Neither Party shall disclose the other Party's Confidential Information to any third party except as expressly permitted under this Agreement.
3.2 Permitted Disclosures. Confidential Information may be disclosed only to those employees, officers, directors, and authorized representatives of the receiving Party (including outside legal counsel and accountants) who (a) have a bona fide need to know such information in connection with the performance of this Agreement or the exercise of rights hereunder; and (b) are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than those set forth in this Section 3. The receiving Party shall be responsible for any unauthorized disclosure by its employees, officers, directors, or authorized representatives.
3.3 Exclusions. The obligations of confidentiality set forth in this Section 3 shall not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement by the receiving Party; (b) was known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure by the disclosing Party, as evidenced by the receiving Party's contemporaneous written records; (c) is independently developed by the receiving Party without reference to or use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information, as demonstrated by documentary evidence; or (d) is lawfully received by the receiving Party from a third party that is not, to the receiving Party's knowledge, subject to any obligation of confidentiality with respect to such information.
3.4 Compelled Disclosure. A receiving Party may disclose the disclosing Party's Confidential Information to the extent required by Applicable Law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or other valid legal process, provided that the receiving Party: (a) gives the disclosing Party prompt written notice of the compelled disclosure to the extent legally permissible, so as to afford the disclosing Party a reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy; (b) cooperates with the disclosing Party, at the disclosing Party's expense, in seeking such protective treatment; and (c) discloses only that portion of the Confidential Information that is legally required to be disclosed.
3.5 Shared Data Set as Confidential Information. The Shared Data Set shall constitute Carden's Confidential Information for all purposes under this Agreement. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, all obligations of Luminos under this Section 3 with respect to Confidential Information shall apply in full to the Shared Data Set.
3.6 Survival. The obligations of confidentiality set forth in this Section 3 shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years from the date of such termination or expiration, regardless of the reason for termination.
SECTION 4 — DATA USE LIMITATION
4.1 Authorized Purpose. Luminos may use the Shared Data Set solely for the creation of aggregated, non-personal Benchmarking Reports for distribution to Luminos's institutional clients (the "Authorized Purpose"). All other uses of the Shared Data Set are expressly prohibited. For the avoidance of doubt, the Authorized Purpose does not include the use of the Shared Data Set for Luminos's own proprietary trading activities, the training of machine-learning models for sale or license to third parties, or the provision of record-level data to any third party.
4.2 Prohibition on Unauthorized Use. Luminos shall not use the Shared Data Set, in whole or in part, for any purpose other than the Authorized Purpose without the prior written consent of Carden, which consent Carden may grant or withhold in its sole discretion. Any request for an expanded use of the Shared Data Set shall be submitted to Carden in writing and shall describe the proposed use in reasonable detail.
4.3 Prohibition on Distribution. Luminos shall not sell, sublicense, rent, lease, distribute, or otherwise make the Shared Data Set (or any record-level extract, subset, or derivative thereof that preserves record-level granularity) available to any third party, whether directly or indirectly, except as expressly permitted under this Agreement. For the avoidance of doubt, the distribution of Benchmarking Reports to Luminos's institutional clients in accordance with the Authorized Purpose shall not constitute a violation of this Section 4.3, provided that such Benchmarking Reports comply with Section 4.4.
4.4 Aggregation Requirements. Any Benchmarking Reports produced by Luminos from the Shared Data Set must be sufficiently aggregated such that no individual consumer and no individual merchant can be identified, directly or indirectly, from the report. Luminos shall implement and maintain appropriate statistical disclosure controls, including minimum cell-size thresholds, suppression rules, and perturbation techniques, to ensure compliance with this requirement. Carden reserves the right to review sample Benchmarking Reports upon reasonable request to verify compliance with this Section 4.4.
SECTION 5 — FEES AND PAYMENT
5.1 Data-Access Fee. In consideration of Carden's provision of the Shared Data Set to Luminos during the Term, Luminos shall pay Carden a quarterly Data-Access Fee of Two Hundred Forty Thousand Dollars ($240,000.00) per calendar quarter. The Data-Access Fee for any partial calendar quarter shall be prorated on a daily basis.
5.2 Invoicing. Carden shall invoice Luminos on the first business day of each calendar quarter during the Term. Each invoice shall reference this Agreement by title and date, identify the applicable calendar quarter, and specify the amount due. Invoices shall be delivered electronically to the billing contact designated by Luminos in writing.
5.3 Payment Terms. All invoices issued pursuant to Section 5.2 are payable within thirty (30) days of Luminos's receipt thereof (net-30). Payment shall be made by wire transfer or automated clearing house (ACH) transfer to the bank account designated by Carden in writing.
5.4 Late Payments. Any amount not paid when due shall accrue interest from the date such payment was due until the date of actual payment at a rate equal to the lesser of (a) one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, compounded monthly, or (b) the maximum rate permitted by Applicable Law. Luminos shall also reimburse Carden for all reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by Carden in connection with the collection of past-due amounts.
5.5 Taxes. All fees set forth in this Agreement are exclusive of any and all applicable taxes, levies, duties, and governmental charges. Luminos shall be solely responsible for the payment of all sales, use, value-added, goods-and-services, withholding, or similar taxes arising from this Agreement, excluding taxes imposed on Carden's net income. If Luminos is required by Applicable Law to withhold any taxes from payments due to Carden, Luminos shall gross up such payments so that Carden receives the full amount of the applicable fee after deduction of all withholding taxes.
SECTION 6 — DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
6.1 Retention Period. Luminos may retain each quarterly delivery of the Shared Data Set for a period not to exceed twelve (12) months from the date of receipt of such delivery. Upon the expiration of the applicable twelve-month retention period, Luminos shall promptly proceed with the deletion or return of the Shared Data Set in accordance with this Section 6.
6.2 Mandatory Deletion. Upon expiration of the twelve-month retention period specified in Section 6.1, Luminos shall securely delete or destroy all copies of the applicable Shared Data Set — including, without limitation, copies residing in production environments, staging environments, development environments, backup systems, archival storage, disaster-recovery environments, and any other media, systems, or repositories — using industry-standard methods consistent with NIST Special Publication 800-88, Revision 1 (Guidelines for Media Sanitization), or an equivalent standard approved by Carden in writing.
6.3 Deletion Certification. Within ten (10) business days following the completion of deletion under Section 6.2, Luminos shall provide Carden with a written certification of destruction (the "Deletion Certificate"), signed by an authorized officer of Luminos, confirming that all copies of the applicable Shared Data Set have been permanently and irreversibly deleted or destroyed in accordance with the requirements of Section 6.2. The Deletion Certificate shall specify the deletion method employed, the date(s) of deletion, and the categories of systems and media from which the Shared Data Set was purged.
6.4 No Anonymization-in-Place. For the avoidance of doubt, anonymization-in-place (i.e., the application of additional de-identification techniques to the Shared Data Set in lieu of deletion) shall not satisfy the deletion obligation under Section 6.2. Only complete and irreversible destruction of the Shared Data Set, or return to Carden in accordance with Section 6.5, is acceptable.
6.5 Return Option. In lieu of deletion under Section 6.2, Luminos may elect to return the Shared Data Set to Carden in a mutually agreed secure format and via a mutually agreed secure transmission method. In such case, the certification required under Section 6.3 shall confirm the return of the Shared Data Set and the absence of any retained copies in any system, medium, or repository under Luminos's custody or control, including the systems of any Subprocessor.
6.6 Survival. The obligations under this Section 6 shall survive any expiration or termination of this Agreement, regardless of the reason for such expiration or termination, and shall remain in full force and effect until fully performed.
SECTION 7 — PROHIBITION ON RE-IDENTIFICATION
7.1 Absolute Prohibition. Luminos shall not, and shall not permit any third party (including any Subprocessor, affiliate, client, consultant, or other person or entity) to, engage in Re-Identification of any element of the Shared Data Set. This prohibition is absolute, unconditional, and applies regardless of the method, technology, data source, or combination thereof used or proposed to be used.
7.2 No Exceptions. No exception to the prohibition set forth in Section 7.1 is permitted under any circumstances, including exceptions characterized as good-faith research, internal data-quality testing, data-validation exercises, benchmarking-accuracy verification, academic inquiry, product-development testing, or any other purpose, regardless of whether such activity is conducted in a controlled or sandboxed environment.
7.3 Material Breach. Any attempt at Re-Identification, whether or not successful, shall constitute a material breach of this Agreement. Upon the occurrence of any such breach, Carden shall be entitled to exercise all remedies set forth in Section 12.3 (including immediate termination without cure period) and any other remedies available at law or in equity, including injunctive relief. The Parties acknowledge and agree that Re-Identification of any element of the Shared Data Set would cause irreparable harm to Carden and to the consumers whose data is contained therein, and that monetary damages alone would be insufficient to compensate Carden for such harm.
7.4 Downstream Obligations. Luminos shall impose an equivalent prohibition on Re-Identification on each Subprocessor approved pursuant to Section 9, by including in the applicable Subprocessor agreement contractual provisions that (a) prohibit the Subprocessor from engaging in Re-Identification in terms no less restrictive than those set forth in this Section 7; and (b) expressly entitle Carden to enforce such prohibition as a third-party beneficiary of the Subprocessor agreement. Luminos shall provide Carden with copies of the relevant provisions of each Subprocessor agreement upon Carden's request.
7.5 Notification of Suspected Re-Identification. If Luminos becomes aware of any actual or suspected Re-Identification of any element of the Shared Data Set, including Re-Identification conducted by, or at the direction of, any Subprocessor, employee, contractor, or agent of Luminos, Luminos shall immediately notify Carden in accordance with the timelines and procedures set forth in Section 8 (Security Incident Notification). Any actual or suspected Re-Identification shall be treated as a Security Incident for purposes of Section 8.
SECTION 8 — SECURITY INCIDENT NOTIFICATION
8.1 Notification Obligation. In the event of a Security Incident involving the Shared Data Set, Luminos shall notify Carden no later than twenty-four (24) hours after Discovery of the Security Incident. Time is of the essence with respect to this notification obligation.
8.2 Trigger. The notification obligation under Section 8.1 is triggered by Discovery, as defined in Section 1.8. For the avoidance of doubt, Luminos may not delay, defer, or withhold notification pending confirmation of the Security Incident, completion of a forensic investigation, validation of the scope or magnitude of the incident, identification of the root cause, completion of an internal investigation, or any other event or milestone. The Parties acknowledge that early notification is essential to enable Carden to protect the interests of affected consumers and to comply with its own regulatory and contractual obligations.
8.3 Recipients. Notification under this Section 8 shall be directed simultaneously to: (a) Carden's Privacy Incident Response team at the contact details specified in Exhibit B; and (b) the VP & Associate General Counsel – Privacy at the contact details specified in Exhibit B. Luminos shall ensure that notification reaches both recipients within the twenty-four (24)-hour period specified in Section 8.1.
8.4 Form of Initial Notification. Initial notification may be provided orally (including by telephone) or by electronic communication (including by e-mail or secure messaging) to the contacts specified in Section 8.3. Oral notification shall be followed by written confirmation within four (4) hours. The initial notification shall include, to the extent known at the time of notification, a preliminary description of the nature of the Security Incident and the Luminos contact person responsible for managing the incident.
8.5 Written Incident Report. Within five (5) business days following the initial notification provided under Section 8.4, Luminos shall deliver to Carden a detailed written incident report (the "Incident Report") describing, at a minimum: (a) the nature and circumstances of the Security Incident, including the date and time of Discovery and the apparent cause; (b) the categories and approximate volume of records within the Shared Data Set that were, or are reasonably believed to have been, affected; (c) the likely consequences of the Security Incident for Carden, the affected consumers, and Carden's merchant network; (d) the measures taken or proposed to be taken by Luminos to contain, mitigate, and remediate the effects of the Security Incident; and (e) the identity, title, and contact information of Luminos's designated point of contact for the incident. Luminos shall supplement the Incident Report promptly as additional information becomes available.
8.6 Ongoing Cooperation. Luminos shall cooperate fully and in good faith with Carden in the investigation, containment, remediation, and resolution of any Security Incident involving the Shared Data Set, including by: (a) preserving all relevant evidence, logs, and forensic artifacts in their original form; (b) providing Carden and its designated representatives (including outside forensic consultants) with reasonable access to relevant systems, networks, facilities, and personnel; (c) complying with reasonable instructions from Carden regarding containment and remediation measures; and (d) participating in joint incident-review meetings as reasonably requested by Carden.
8.7 No Independent Consumer Notification. Luminos shall not issue any notification, communication, or public statement to affected consumers, regulatory authorities, law enforcement, the media, or any other third party regarding a Security Incident involving the Shared Data Set without Carden's prior written approval, unless Luminos is compelled to do so by Applicable Law. In the event that Luminos is compelled by Applicable Law to issue such notification, Luminos shall (a) provide Carden with as much advance notice as is practicable under the circumstances; (b) afford Carden a reasonable opportunity to review and comment on the content of the notification; and (c) incorporate Carden's reasonable comments to the extent consistent with Applicable Law.
SECTION 9 — SUBPROCESSORS
9.1 Prior Written Consent Required. Luminos shall not engage any Subprocessor to access, store, process, transmit, analyze, or otherwise handle any portion of the Shared Data Set without obtaining Carden's prior written consent. Carden may withhold such consent in its sole and absolute discretion, and shall have no obligation to provide a reason for any denial. As of the Effective Date, Luminos has not engaged, and is not authorized to engage, any Subprocessor with respect to the Shared Data Set.
9.2 Vendor-Security Assessment. Before Carden will consider granting consent under Section 9.1, the proposed Subprocessor must undergo and satisfactorily complete Carden's Vendor-Security Assessment. Carden's Information Security team shall administer the VSA in accordance with Carden's then-current policies and procedures. Luminos shall cause the proposed Subprocessor to cooperate fully with the assessment process, including by providing timely and complete responses to questionnaires, granting access to relevant documentation and certifications (such as SOC 2 Type II reports, ISO 27001 certificates, and penetration-test summaries), and making security personnel available for interviews as reasonably requested by Carden. Carden shall use commercially reasonable efforts to complete the VSA within forty-five (45) days of receipt of all required information from the proposed Subprocessor.
9.3 Subprocessor Agreement. Upon Carden's written approval of a proposed Subprocessor, Luminos shall, prior to providing the Subprocessor with access to any portion of the Shared Data Set, enter into a written agreement with such Subprocessor (the "Subprocessor Agreement") that imposes data-protection, security, confidentiality, re-identification prohibition, use-limitation, data-retention-and-deletion, and audit obligations no less protective than those imposed on Luminos under this Agreement. Luminos shall provide Carden with a copy of the executed Subprocessor Agreement, or the relevant data-protection provisions thereof, promptly upon Carden's request.
9.4 Ongoing Liability. Luminos shall remain fully liable to Carden for all acts, omissions, failures, and breaches of each approved Subprocessor with respect to the Shared Data Set, as though such acts, omissions, failures, or breaches were those of Luminos. The engagement of a Subprocessor shall not relieve Luminos of any of its obligations under this Agreement.
9.5 Revocation of Consent. Carden reserves the right to revoke its consent to any approved Subprocessor upon written notice to Luminos if Carden, in its reasonable judgment, identifies material deficiencies in the Subprocessor's security posture, compliance practices, or operational capabilities, or if the Subprocessor experiences a Security Incident affecting Carden data. Upon receipt of a revocation notice, Luminos shall: (a) immediately cease providing the Shared Data Set to the affected Subprocessor; (b) cause the Subprocessor to cease all processing of the Shared Data Set within fifteen (15) days; and (c) cause the Subprocessor to securely delete all copies of the Shared Data Set in its custody or control within thirty (30) days, with a written certification of deletion provided to Carden.
9.6 Notice of Change. Luminos shall notify Carden promptly, and in any event within ten (10) business days, of any material change in the ownership, control, management, security practices, or data-processing operations of any approved Subprocessor. Such notification shall include a reasonably detailed description of the change and its potential impact on the Subprocessor's handling of the Shared Data Set. Carden may, following receipt of such notification, require the Subprocessor to undergo a supplemental VSA and may revoke its consent in accordance with Section 9.5 if the results of the supplemental assessment are unsatisfactory.
SECTION 10 — AUDIT RIGHTS
10.1 Audit Right. Carden shall have the right to conduct audits of Luminos's data-handling, data-security, data-privacy, and data-retention practices as they relate to the Shared Data Set. Audits may include, without limitation, on-site inspections at Luminos's facilities, remote assessments (such as documentation reviews, questionnaire-based assessments, and remote system testing), and reviews of relevant policies, procedures, access logs, system configurations, and personnel-training records. Carden may conduct such audits using its own personnel or may engage qualified third-party auditors, provided that any such third-party auditors are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those set forth in Section 3.
10.2 Frequency. Carden may exercise its audit right under Section 10.1 up to once per calendar year during the Term and for a period of one (1) year following the expiration or termination of this Agreement. Notwithstanding the foregoing frequency limitation, Carden may conduct additional audits at any time following the occurrence of a Security Incident, a suspected breach of this Agreement, or a revocation of Subprocessor consent under Section 9.5, without regard to whether an annual audit has already been conducted in the applicable calendar year.
10.3 Notice. Carden shall provide Luminos with at least fifteen (15) business days' prior written notice of an intended audit, specifying the proposed scope, anticipated duration, and the personnel or third-party auditors who will be conducting the audit. In the case of an audit triggered by a Security Incident or suspected breach, Carden shall provide as much advance notice as is reasonably practicable under the circumstances, but in no event fewer than three (3) business days.
10.4 Cooperation. Luminos shall cooperate fully and in good faith with each audit conducted under this Section 10, including by: (a) providing reasonable access to its facilities, systems, networks, records, and relevant personnel during normal business hours (or at other mutually agreed times); (b) responding to audit inquiries and information requests in a timely and complete manner; (c) making available copies of relevant policies, procedures, certifications, and third-party audit reports (including SOC 2 reports); and (d) designating a primary point of contact to coordinate with Carden's audit team.
10.5 Cost Allocation. The costs of audits conducted under this Section 10 shall be borne by Carden; provided, however, that if an audit reveals a material breach of this Agreement or a material deficiency in Luminos's security practices, data-handling procedures, or compliance with the terms hereof, Luminos shall reimburse Carden for the reasonable and documented costs of that audit, including the fees and expenses of any third-party auditors engaged by Carden. Luminos shall remit such reimbursement within thirty (30) days of receipt of an invoice from Carden.
10.6 Regulatory Disclosure. Nothing in this Agreement shall restrict, condition, or require Luminos's consent for Carden's disclosure of audit findings, compliance reports, remediation plans, or related documentation to any applicable regulatory authority, including federal and state banking regulators, data-protection authorities, and consumer-protection agencies. Carden's right to share such information with regulators is absolute and non-waivable, and Luminos hereby acknowledges and consents to such disclosure.
10.7 Remediation. If an audit conducted under this Section 10 identifies deficiencies in Luminos's practices, controls, or compliance, Luminos shall prepare and deliver to Carden a written remediation plan within fifteen (15) business days of receipt of Carden's written audit findings. The remediation plan shall describe in reasonable detail the specific actions Luminos will take to address each identified deficiency, the responsible personnel, and the proposed timeline for implementation. Luminos shall implement the remediation plan within the timeframe mutually agreed by the Parties, and Carden shall have the right to verify completion of the remediation through a follow-up assessment.
SECTION 11 — CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS
11.1 General Restriction. Luminos shall not transfer, transmit, remotely access, or otherwise make the Shared Data Set (or any portion thereof) accessible from outside the United States, including to any Luminos affiliate, Subprocessor, contractor, or other third party located outside the United States, or through any system, server, or infrastructure located outside the United States, unless all three of the following prerequisites have been satisfied in full:
(a) Prior Written Approval. Carden's VP & Associate General Counsel – Privacy (or such other officer as Carden may designate in writing from time to time) has provided express written authorization for the specific proposed transfer, identifying the destination jurisdiction, the importing entity, and the categories of data to be transferred;
(b) Transfer Impact Assessment. Carden has completed a Transfer Impact Assessment for the destination jurisdiction, and the results of the TIA are satisfactory to Carden in its reasonable discretion. The TIA shall evaluate, at a minimum, the legal framework of the destination jurisdiction, the existence and enforcement of data-protection laws, the scope of governmental surveillance and access powers, and the availability of effective legal remedies for data subjects; and
(c) Standard Contractual Clauses. The Parties (and, where applicable, the importing entity in the destination jurisdiction) have executed the applicable European Union Standard Contractual Clauses (as adopted by the European Commission) and/or United Kingdom International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or such other transfer mechanism as Carden deems adequate in its reasonable discretion, and all supplementary measures identified in the TIA have been implemented.
11.2 SCCs Alone Insufficient. For the avoidance of doubt, the execution of Standard Contractual Clauses or any other contractual transfer mechanism alone, without the concurrent satisfaction of prerequisites (a) and (b) of Section 11.1, shall not authorize any cross-border transfer of the Shared Data Set.
11.3 Individual Assessment. Each proposed transfer destination and each proposed importing entity must be individually assessed under Section 11.1. Blanket approvals or framework authorizations covering multiple jurisdictions, multiple affiliates, or multiple Subprocessors are not permitted. Any change in the importing entity, the destination jurisdiction, or the scope of the proposed transfer shall require a new assessment and new written approval.
11.4 Obligation to Inform. Luminos shall notify Carden in writing at least thirty (30) days in advance of any proposed cross-border transfer of the Shared Data Set, providing sufficient detail to enable Carden to evaluate the request and conduct the TIA. Such notification shall include, at a minimum: (a) the identity and legal name of the proposed importing entity; (b) the destination jurisdiction; (c) the categories and approximate volume of Shared Data Set to be transferred; (d) the purpose of the transfer; (e) the proposed transfer mechanism; and (f) a description of the technical and organizational safeguards that will be applied to the data in the destination jurisdiction.

EXHIBIT A
Description of Shared Data Set and Delivery Specifications
This Exhibit A is attached to and incorporated into the Data Sharing Agreement dated as of April 14, 2025, by and between Carden Analytics, Inc. and Luminos Insights Group, LLC (the "Agreement"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Exhibit A shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Agreement.
1. Description of the Shared Data Set
The Shared Data Set consists of pseudonymized consumer point-of-sale transaction records originating from Carden's merchant network. Each record within the Shared Data Set may include the following categories of data fields:
Field Category | Description
Pseudonymized Consumer Identifier | A unique, non-reversible token assigned by Carden to each consumer, replacing all directly identifying information (e.g., name, account number, payment-card number)
Transaction Date | The calendar date on which the transaction was processed
Transaction Time | The approximate time of the transaction (rounded to the nearest hour)
Transaction Amount | The total monetary value of the transaction in U.S. dollars
Merchant Category Code (MCC) | The standardized ISO 18245 merchant category code associated with the merchant
Merchant Region | A geographic descriptor limited to the metropolitan statistical area (MSA) or state level; no street-level or ZIP-code-level merchant location data is included
Transaction Type | The classification of the transaction (e.g., in-store, online, mobile)
Payment Channel | A high-level indicator of the payment method used (e.g., credit, debit, prepaid)

For the avoidance of doubt, the Shared Data Set does not include: (a) consumer names, addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, Social Security numbers, or other direct identifiers; (b) raw payment-card numbers or account numbers; (c) merchant names or merchant-level identifiers that could be used to identify a specific merchant location; or (d) any data field that, standing alone, would constitute personal information under Applicable Law.
2. Estimated Volume
The Shared Data Set is estimated at approximately thirty-eight million (38,000,000) records per calendar quarter. Actual volumes may vary depending on transaction activity within Carden's merchant network and are subject to change without amendment to this Agreement; provided, however, that Carden shall notify Luminos if the quarterly volume is expected to vary by more than fifteen percent (15%) from the stated estimate.
3. Delivery Cadence
The Shared Data Set shall be delivered on a quarterly basis, within thirty (30) calendar days following the end of each calendar quarter (i.e., delivery for Q1 by April 30, Q2 by July 30, Q3 by October 30, and Q4 by January 30 of the following year).
4. Secure Transmission Method and Format
Specification | Detail
File Format | Apache Parquet (columnar format), compressed using Snappy compression
Transmission Method | Secure File Transfer Protocol (SFTP) over a dedicated, encrypted channel, or upload to a mutually agreed cloud-based secure data exchange environment (e.g., AWS S3 bucket with server-side encryption using AES-256 and access restricted by IAM policies)
Encryption in Transit | TLS 1.2 or higher
Encryption at Rest | AES-256 encryption applied prior to transmission; Luminos shall maintain AES-256 encryption at rest for all copies of the Shared Data Set in its custody
Authentication | Multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for all access to the SFTP endpoint or cloud storage environment
Integrity Verification | Each delivery shall be accompanied by a SHA-256 hash file to enable Luminos to verify data integrity upon receipt

Carden and Luminos shall coordinate on the initial technical setup and testing of the secure transmission channel prior to the first quarterly delivery. Any changes to the format or transmission specifications set forth in this Exhibit A shall be agreed upon in writing by both Parties.
