ARTICLE 4 — FEES AND PAYMENT
Section 4.1 — Annual License Fee. In consideration of the license granted under Section 2.1, Licensee shall pay to Licensor annual license fees in the amounts specified in Schedule A. The annual license fee for Year 1 of the Initial Term (i.e., the twelve (12)-month period commencing on the Effective Date) shall be Two Million Six Hundred Forty Thousand Dollars ($2,640,000.00). Annual license fees for subsequent years of the Term shall be calculated in accordance with Section 4.2 and as illustrated in Schedule A.
Section 4.2 — Fee Escalator. Beginning on the first anniversary of the Effective Date (Year 2) and on each anniversary thereafter during the Term, the annual license fee shall automatically increase by four percent (4%) per annum, compounding on the prior year's annual license fee. By way of illustration and not limitation, the Year 2 annual license fee shall be $2,745,600.00 (i.e., $2,640,000.00 × 1.04), the Year 3 annual license fee shall be $2,855,424.00 (i.e., $2,745,600.00 × 1.04), and so forth as further detailed in Schedule A, Section A.4. For any Renewal Term, the annual license fee shall continue to escalate at the same four percent (4%) per annum rate, compounding on the annual license fee in effect during the final year of the immediately preceding Term.
Section 4.3 — Payment Terms. All annual license fees shall be invoiced and payable quarterly in advance. Licensor shall issue invoices to Licensee at least fifteen (15) days before the start of each calendar quarter (or, for the first quarter, at least fifteen (15) days before the Effective Date or as soon as reasonably practicable following execution of this Agreement). Each quarterly installment shall equal one-fourth (1/4) of the applicable annual license fee. By way of example, the quarterly installment for Year 1 shall be Six Hundred Sixty Thousand Dollars ($660,000.00). All payments shall be due and payable net thirty (30) days from the date of the applicable invoice. All payments shall be made in United States dollars by wire transfer or automated clearing house (ACH) transfer to such account as Licensor may designate in writing from time to time.
Section 4.4 — Implementation Services Fees. Fees for Implementation Services shall be as set forth in the applicable Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified in the applicable SOW, Implementation Services fees shall be invoiced monthly in arrears based upon the completion and acceptance (or deemed acceptance) of the milestones identified in the SOW. Licensee shall pay all undisputed Implementation Services invoices within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice.
Section 4.5 — Taxes. All Fees stated in this Agreement and in Schedule A are exclusive of any and all taxes, duties, levies, and similar governmental charges. Licensee shall be responsible for and shall pay all sales, use, value-added, goods and services, excise, withholding, and other taxes and duties imposed by any federal, state, local, or foreign governmental authority in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, excluding only taxes imposed on or measured by Licensor's net income, franchise taxes imposed on Licensor, or taxes arising from Licensor's employment relationship with its own personnel. If Licensee is required by applicable Law to deduct or withhold any taxes from amounts payable to Licensor, Licensee shall gross up such payments so that Licensor receives the full amount of the Fees net of any such withholding.
Section 4.6 — Late Payments. Any amount not paid when due shall bear interest from the due date until paid at the lesser of (a) one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month (eighteen percent (18%) per annum), or (b) the maximum rate permitted by applicable Law. The accrual of interest under this Section 4.6 shall not limit or waive any other right or remedy available to Licensor under this Agreement or at law or in equity. In addition, if any undisputed amount remains overdue for more than fifteen (15) days following written notice from Licensor to Licensee specifying such overdue amount, Licensor may, in its sole discretion and without liability, suspend Licensee's and all Named Users' access to the Platform until all overdue amounts (including accrued interest) have been paid in full. Any such suspension shall not relieve Licensee of its obligation to pay Fees during the period of suspension.
Section 4.7 — Fee Disputes. If Licensee disputes in good faith any portion of an invoice, Licensee shall notify Licensor in writing of such dispute within the applicable payment period, specifying in reasonable detail the amount disputed and the basis for the dispute. All undisputed amounts shall remain due and payable in accordance with the payment terms set forth in this Article 4, notwithstanding the existence of a dispute with respect to any other portion of the invoice. The Parties shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve any fee dispute within thirty (30) days following Licensor's receipt of Licensee's written dispute notice. If the Parties are unable to resolve the dispute within such period, either Party may pursue the remedies available under this Agreement or at law or in equity. Upon resolution of a fee dispute, any amounts determined to be owed shall be paid within fifteen (15) days of such resolution, together with interest thereon at the rate specified in Section 4.6 from the original due date.

Section B.5 — Disaster Recovery and Business Continuity. Licensor shall maintain disaster recovery and business continuity capabilities for the Platform, including the following:
(a) Recovery Point Objective (RPO). Licensor shall maintain a recovery point objective of no greater than four (4) hours, meaning that in the event of a disaster or system failure, the maximum data loss shall not exceed the data generated or modified within the four (4)-hour period immediately preceding the disaster or failure event.
(b) Recovery Time Objective (RTO). Licensor shall maintain a recovery time objective of no greater than twelve (12) hours, meaning that in the event of a disaster or system failure, Licensor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to restore the Platform to operational status within twelve (12) hours of the declaration of a disaster event.
(c) Geographically Separate Backup Facilities. Licensor shall maintain backup and failover infrastructure in data center facilities that are geographically separate from the primary data center facilities used to host the Platform in production. Such backup facilities shall be located at a sufficient distance from the primary facilities to mitigate the risk of a single regional event affecting both the primary and backup environments.
(d) Testing. Licensor shall test its disaster recovery and business continuity plans at least once per calendar year and shall make a summary of the test results available to Licensee upon written request.
