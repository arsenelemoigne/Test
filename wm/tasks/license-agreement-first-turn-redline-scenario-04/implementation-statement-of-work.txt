SECTION 1 — BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVES
1.1 Purpose.  The purpose of this SOW is to define the scope, responsibilities, milestones, deliverables, fees, and other terms governing the implementation and deployment of the Veridian OnePlatform (the "Platform") across Licensee's North American operations (the "Implementation Services"). The Implementation Services encompass the full lifecycle of activities necessary to achieve production go-live readiness, including, without limitation, the following workstreams: (a) discovery and current-state assessment; (b) functional and technical design; (c) Platform configuration; (d) data migration from Licensee's existing legacy systems; (e) integration of the Platform with Licensee's identified third-party systems; (f) system integration testing, user acceptance testing, and performance testing; (g) end-user and administrator training; and (h) production go-live and post-go-live hypercare support.
This SOW, together with its exhibits and any Change Orders executed hereunder, constitutes the complete description of the Implementation Services to be performed by Licensor for Licensee under the Agreement.
1.2 Business Context
Halcyon Industrial Holdings, LLC is a specialty chemicals manufacturer operating multiple production facilities, distribution centers, and administrative offices across North America. Licensee's current operational technology landscape comprises a heterogeneous collection of legacy enterprise resource planning ("ERP") systems, point solutions, and manual processes that have been accumulated through organic growth and prior acquisitions. This fragmented landscape results in duplicative data entry, inconsistent business processes, limited visibility into cross-facility operations, and elevated compliance risk.
The deployment of the Veridian OnePlatform is intended to consolidate and standardize Licensee's core ERP functions across all of Licensee's North American facilities, thereby achieving a single, unified platform for the management of the following functional areas:
(a) Finance and Accounting, including general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, fixed assets, and financial consolidation;
(b) Supply Chain Management, including demand planning, procurement requisitioning, and supplier collaboration;
(c) Manufacturing Execution, including production scheduling, shop floor control, batch processing, and quality management;
(d) Inventory and Warehouse Management, including real-time inventory tracking, warehouse operations, and shipping/receiving;
(e) Procurement, including strategic sourcing, purchase order management, and contract management;
(f) Human Capital Management, including core HR, payroll, benefits administration, time and attendance, and talent management; and
(g) Reporting and Analytics, including operational dashboards, financial reporting, and executive-level business intelligence.
By consolidating these functions onto a single platform, Licensee expects to achieve improved operational efficiency, enhanced data integrity, stronger regulatory compliance posture, and more timely and accurate decision-making across its organization.
1.3 Key Objectives
The key objectives of the Implementation Services described in this SOW are as follows:
(a) Deployment of Core Platform Modules. Deploy the core Platform modules identified in Section 2.1 to support up to one thousand five hundred (1,500) Named Users, consistent with the Named User Cap set forth in Schedule A of the Agreement, across Licensee's in-scope North American facilities identified in Exhibit A to this SOW.
(b) Legacy Data Migration. Extract, transform, and load designated categories of legacy data from Licensee's existing systems into the Platform, including master data, open transactional data, and historical transactional data, as further described in Section 2.4, in a manner that preserves data integrity and supports Licensee's ongoing business operations and regulatory obligations.
(c) Third-Party System Integration. Design, develop, test, and deploy integration connectors between the Platform and Licensee's identified third-party systems, as further described in Section 2.3, using the Platform's standard application programming interfaces ("APIs") and integration framework per Schedule B of the Agreement.
(d) Production Go-Live Readiness. Achieve production go-live readiness for the Platform within the implementation timeline set forth in Section 4 of this SOW, including the successful completion of all testing, training, and cutover activities necessary to support Licensee's transition from legacy systems to the Platform in a controlled and risk-managed manner.

2.3 In-Scope Integrations
The Implementation Services include the design, development, testing, and deployment of integration connectors between the Platform and the following categories of Licensee third-party systems (collectively, the "In-Scope Integrations"):
(a) Laboratory Information Management System ("LIMS"). Integration with Licensee's existing LIMS to enable bi-directional data exchange of quality testing results, batch release data, certificates of analysis, and material specifications between the LIMS and the Platform's Manufacturing Execution and Quality Management modules.
(b) Transportation Management System ("TMS"). Integration with Licensee's existing TMS to enable the exchange of shipment planning data, carrier assignments, freight cost data, and shipment status updates between the TMS and the Platform's Supply Chain Management and Inventory & Warehouse Management modules.
(c) Corporate Banking and Payment Platforms. Integration with Licensee's corporate banking partners and electronic payment platforms to enable automated payment file generation, bank statement imports, payment status reconciliation, and cash management data exchange between Licensee's banking infrastructure and the Platform's Finance & Accounting module.
(d) Other Integrations. Any additional third-party system integrations identified during the Discovery Phase (Phase 1) that are mutually agreed upon by the Parties for inclusion in the implementation scope, subject to the Change Order process to the extent such additional integrations materially impact the fees, timeline, or resource requirements set forth in this SOW.
All In-Scope Integrations will be developed using the Platform's standard APIs and integration framework as described in Schedule B of the Agreement. Licensor will be responsible for the design, development, and testing of the Platform-side integration connectors. Licensee will be responsible for any configuration or modification required on the third-party system side to enable data exchange with the Platform, except as otherwise specifically agreed in writing.
2.4 Data Migration
The Implementation Services include the extraction, transformation, and loading ("ETL") of designated categories of Licensee legacy data from Licensee's existing systems into the Platform (the "Data Migration"). The Data Migration scope encompasses the following categories of data:
(a) Master Data. Customer master records, vendor/supplier master records, material master records (including chemical compound identifiers, hazard classifications, and regulatory attributes), bills of materials, routings, chart of accounts, cost centers, profit centers, organizational hierarchy structures, and employee master records.
(b) Open Transactional Data. Open purchase orders, open sales orders, open production orders, open accounts payable invoices, open accounts receivable invoices, open inventory commitments, and other in-flight transactions that must be migrated to ensure continuity of business operations at the time of go-live cutover.
(c) Historical Transactional Data. Completed transactional data for a historical period to be mutually agreed upon by the Parties during the Discovery Phase (Phase 1), based on Licensee's business requirements, regulatory retention obligations, and data volume considerations. Unless otherwise agreed, the target historical migration period is anticipated to be twenty-four (24) months of transactional history preceding the go-live date.
Licensor will develop and execute data migration scripts to perform the ETL processes. Licensee will be responsible for providing Licensor with access to legacy systems and data, performing data cleansing and enrichment activities as mutually agreed, and validating the accuracy and completeness of migrated data at each stage of the migration process. The Data Migration will include a minimum of two (2) mock conversion runs prior to the final cutover conversion, as described in Section 4.

Defect Severity Classifications. For purposes of this SOW, defects identified during testing shall be classified using the following severity levels:
•  Severity 1 (Critical): A defect that causes a complete system failure, data loss, or prevents execution of a critical business process with no available workaround.
•  Severity 2 (High): A defect that causes a major functional impact to a business process with no reasonable workaround available; the affected function operates in a significantly degraded manner.
•  Severity 3 (Medium): A defect that causes a moderate functional impact but a reasonable workaround exists; the affected function operates with reduced capability.
•  Severity 4 (Low): A defect that causes a minor or cosmetic issue with no material impact on business process execution.
UAT exit criteria require the remediation of all Severity 1 and Severity 2 defects. Severity 3 and Severity 4 defects may, at Licensee's discretion, be deferred to post-go-live resolution, provided such deferral is documented and approved by the JSC.
