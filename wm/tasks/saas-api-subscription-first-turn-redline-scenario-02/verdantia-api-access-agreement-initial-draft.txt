Article 4 — Rate Limits and Usage Tiers
Section 4.1 — Base Tier
Subject to Licensee's payment of the Annual License Fee, Licensee is entitled to make up to five million (5,000,000) API Calls per calendar month under the Base Tier, as further described in Schedule A. The Base Tier allocation resets at the beginning of each calendar month at 12:00 AM Eastern Time. Unused API Calls from any calendar month shall not carry over to any subsequent month.
Section 4.2 — Overage
API Calls made by Licensee in excess of the Base Tier allocation in any calendar month shall be invoiced at the Overage Rate of four-tenths of one cent ($0.004) per API Call. Overage amounts shall be calculated on a calendar-month basis based on Verdantia's metering systems, which shall be the authoritative source for determining API Call volumes. Overage charges shall be invoiced in arrears and included as a separate line item on the next Quarterly Invoice following the month in which the overage occurred. By way of example, if Licensee makes 5,500,000 API Calls in a given calendar month, the overage charge for that month would be 500,000 × $0.004 = $2,000.
Section 4.3 — Burst Allowance
Licensee may burst up to one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Base Tier rate in any rolling sixty (60) minute window without penalty (the "Burst Allowance"). The Burst Allowance is intended to accommodate temporary spikes in API Call volume associated with batch processing, peak operational periods, or other short-duration increases in demand. API Calls made within the Burst Allowance shall count toward the monthly Base Tier allocation and, if the Base Tier is exceeded, shall be subject to the Overage Rate set forth in Section 4.2.
API Calls that exceed the Burst Allowance in any rolling sixty (60) minute window shall be subject to throttling by Verdantia's systems. Upon throttling, Verdantia's systems will return HTTP 429 (Too Many Requests) responses for API Calls exceeding the Burst Allowance until the Licensee's request rate falls below the Burst Allowance threshold. No SLA Credit shall be issued, and Verdantia shall have no liability, for throttle events caused by Licensee exceeding the Burst Allowance.
Section 4.4 — Monitoring and Reporting
Verdantia shall make usage dashboards available to Licensee through Verdantia's online portal, showing real-time and historical API Call volumes, including daily, weekly, and monthly usage summaries, Burst Allowance utilization, overage tracking, error rates, and latency metrics. Verdantia shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that usage data displayed on the dashboard is current within a fifteen (15) minute delay from the time of the relevant API Call. Licensee shall have the right to export usage data from the dashboard in CSV or JSON format for its internal record-keeping purposes.
In the event of any discrepancy between Licensee's internal usage records and Verdantia's metering systems, Verdantia's metering systems shall be presumed correct absent manifest error. Licensee may request a detailed usage audit for any calendar month by providing written notice to Verdantia within thirty (30) days following the end of the month in question. Verdantia shall cooperate in good faith with any such audit request and shall provide Licensee with detailed API Call logs for the relevant period within fifteen (15) business days of receiving such request.

Article 6 — Service Levels and Uptime
Section 6.1 — Uptime Commitment
Verdantia commits to providing Uptime Availability of ninety-nine and nine-tenths percent (99.9%) for the Verdantia Nexus API, measured on a calendar month basis in accordance with the calculation methodology set forth in Exhibit B. The 99.9% Uptime Availability commitment permits approximately forty-three and two-tenths (43.2) minutes of total Downtime per calendar month (based on a thirty (30) day month). Verdantia shall use commercially reasonable efforts to exceed the 99.9% Uptime Availability commitment and to minimize the duration and frequency of any Downtime events.
Section 6.2 — Scheduled Maintenance
Verdantia may perform Scheduled Maintenance on the Verdantia Nexus API and its supporting infrastructure for up to four (4) hours per calendar month. Verdantia shall provide Licensee with not less than seventy-two (72) hours' prior written notice of each Scheduled Maintenance window, specifying the anticipated start time, duration, and scope of the maintenance. Verdantia shall use commercially reasonable efforts to schedule maintenance during off-peak hours, defined as weekends or weekday hours between 12:00 AM and 6:00 AM Eastern Time, to minimize disruption to Licensee's operations. Scheduled Maintenance windows that comply with the notice and duration requirements of this Section 6.2 shall not count toward Downtime for purposes of calculating Uptime Availability. In the event that Scheduled Maintenance exceeds the four (4) hour monthly allocation or is performed without the required seventy-two (72) hours' prior written notice, the excess time or unnoticed maintenance period shall be treated as Downtime.
Section 6.3 — SLA Credits
If Verdantia fails to meet the 99.9% Uptime Availability commitment in any given calendar month, Licensee shall be entitled to SLA Credits calculated as follows:
(a) For each one-tenth of one percent (0.1%) of Uptime Availability below 99.9%, Licensee shall receive a credit equal to five percent (5%) of the Monthly Fee of one hundred five thousand dollars ($105,000), resulting in a credit of five thousand two hundred fifty dollars ($5,250) per 0.1% shortfall.
(b) SLA Credits in any single calendar month shall not exceed thirty percent (30%) of the Monthly Fee, representing a maximum SLA Credit of thirty-one thousand five hundred dollars ($31,500) per calendar month.
(c) SLA Credits shall be applied as a credit against the next Quarterly Invoice following the determination of the credit amount. In the event that no future invoices are anticipated (e.g., due to impending termination of the Agreement), Verdantia shall, upon Licensee's written request, refund the applicable SLA Credit amount to Licensee within thirty (30) days.
The SLA Credit schedule is set forth in detail in the SLA Credit Calculation table in Exhibit B. SLA Credits are Licensee's sole and exclusive remedy, and Verdantia's sole and exclusive liability, for any failure by Verdantia to meet the Uptime Availability commitment set forth in this Article 6.
Section 6.4 — Exclusions
SLA Credits shall not be available, and Verdantia shall have no liability under this Article 6, with respect to any Downtime or service degradation resulting from:
(a) Scheduled Maintenance performed in accordance with Section 6.2;
(b) Licensee's acts, omissions, or failures, including without limitation misuse of the API, errors in Hollowell Input Data, or failures in Licensee's systems, networks, or infrastructure;
(c) force majeure events as described in Article 18;
(d) throttling resulting from Licensee exceeding the Burst Allowance as described in Section 4.3;
(e) failures, outages, or degradation in third-party systems, networks, infrastructure, or services that are outside Verdantia's reasonable control, including internet backbone outages, DNS failures, or cloud provider outages; or
(f) suspension of access pursuant to Section 5.4 (late payment) or Section 3.2 (use restriction violations).
Section 6.5 — Credit Request Procedure
To receive SLA Credits, Licensee must submit a written SLA Credit request to Verdantia within thirty (30) calendar days following the end of the calendar month in which the Downtime giving rise to the credit request occurred. Each credit request shall include: (i) the date(s) and time(s) of the Downtime event(s); (ii) the duration of each Downtime event; (iii) a description of the impact on Licensee's use of the Verdantia Nexus API; and (iv) any supporting evidence, including error logs, screenshots, or monitoring data, reasonably available to Licensee. Verdantia shall review each credit request and respond in writing within fifteen (15) business days, confirming or disputing the request with reasonable supporting detail. Failure by Licensee to submit a credit request within the thirty (30) day period shall constitute a waiver of Licensee's right to SLA Credits for the applicable month. Detailed procedures for submitting and processing SLA Credit requests are set forth in Exhibit B.
Article 7 — Security Obligations

(c) network security measures, including firewalls, intrusion detection and prevention systems, and vulnerability scanning;
(d) regular security assessments, including penetration testing conducted at least annually by a qualified third party;

(g) secure software development practices, including code review, security testing, and vulnerability management.

This Exhibit A is attached to and forms an integral part of the API Access and Use Agreement (the "Agreement") between Verdantia Systems Inc. A.1 — Product Name
Verdantia Nexus API
A.2 — Overview
The Verdantia Nexus API is Verdantia's proprietary application programming interface designed to provide supply-chain data enrichment, analytics, predictive modeling, and integration services. The Verdantia Nexus API enables Licensee to submit supply-chain data, logistics data, inventory data, and transactional data for processing by Verdantia's systems and to receive enriched, analyzed, and aggregated data outputs in return.
Authentication. The Verdantia Nexus API supports the following authentication mechanisms:
(a) OAuth 2.0 — Client credentials grant flow for server-to-server authentication. Licensee will be issued a Client ID and Client Secret upon execution of this Agreement. Access tokens are issued by Verdantia's authorization server and have a configurable expiration period (default: 3,600 seconds / 1 hour).
(b) API Key Authentication — Static API key authentication for legacy integrations. API keys are issued by Verdantia upon request and must be included in the HTTP header of each API Call. API key authentication is supported but OAuth 2.0 is the recommended and preferred authentication method.
Supported Data Formats. The Verdantia Nexus API supports the following data interchange formats:
•  JSON (JavaScript Object Notation) — Primary and recommended format for request and response payloads.
•  XML (Extensible Markup Language) — Supported for request and response payloads upon configuration.
Protocol. All API communications shall be conducted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher). Unencrypted HTTP connections are not supported and will be rejected.
Integration Guidance. Verdantia shall provide Licensee with technical integration guidance, including sample code, SDK libraries (where available), and implementation best practices, to facilitate the integration of the Verdantia Nexus API with the Hollowell SCM Platform.
A.3 — Endpoints
The Verdantia Nexus API provides the following categories of endpoints (detailed endpoint specifications, including request/response schemas, are maintained in Verdantia's online developer documentation portal):
(a) /v2/supply-chain/enrich — Supply chain data enrichment endpoint. Accepts Hollowell Input Data and returns enriched data outputs.
(b) /v2/analytics/query — Analytics query endpoint. Supports parameterized queries against Verdantia's analytics engine.
(c) /v2/predict/forecast — Predictive forecasting endpoint. Accepts historical data and configuration parameters and returns demand and supply forecasts.
(d) /v2/inventory/optimize — Inventory optimization endpoint. Accepts inventory data and returns optimization recommendations.
(e) /v2/logistics/route — Logistics routing endpoint. Accepts origin/destination and constraint data and returns optimized routing recommendations.
(f) /v2/status — Health check and status endpoint. Returns current API status, version, and system health information.
(g) /v2/usage — Usage reporting endpoint. Returns current API Call counts, rate limit status, and Burst Allowance utilization for the authenticated Licensee.

Verdantia commits to providing ninety-nine and nine-tenths percent (99.9%) monthly Uptime Availability for the Verdantia Nexus API. Uptime Availability is calculated using the following formula:
Uptime Availability (%) = ((Total Minutes in Calendar Month - Downtime Minutes) / (Total Minutes in Calendar Month - Scheduled Maintenance Minutes)) x 100
Where:
•  Total Minutes in Calendar Month = the total number of minutes in the applicable calendar month (e.g., 43,200 minutes for a 30-day month; 44,640 minutes for a 31-day month).
•  Downtime Minutes = the total number of minutes during the calendar month in which the Verdantia Nexus API was unavailable, as measured by Verdantia's monitoring systems, excluding Scheduled Maintenance.
•  Scheduled Maintenance Minutes = the total number of minutes of Scheduled Maintenance performed during the calendar month in accordance with Section B.2.
The 99.9% Uptime Availability commitment permits approximately 43.2 minutes of total Downtime per calendar month (based on a 30-day month, excluding Scheduled Maintenance). For a 31-day month, the permitted Downtime is approximately 44.6 minutes.

(d) Exclusion from Downtime. Scheduled Maintenance windows that comply with the notice requirements of this Section B.2 shall not be counted as Downtime for purposes of calculating Uptime Availability.

(a) the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and any applicable national implementing legislation, to the extent that Hollowell Input Data contains personal data of data subjects located in the European Economic Area;
(b) the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the UK Data Protection Act 2018, to the extent that Hollowell Input Data contains personal data of data subjects located in the United Kingdom;
(c) the California Consumer Privacy Act, Cal. Civ. Code Section 1798.100 et seq., as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 ("CCPA/CPRA"), to the extent that Hollowell Input Data contains personal information of California consumers;
(d) any other applicable federal, state, local, or foreign data protection, data privacy, or data security laws, rules, or regulations that apply to the processing of personal data under this Agreement.
In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the Agreement with respect to the processing of personal data, the terms of this DPA shall prevail and control.
