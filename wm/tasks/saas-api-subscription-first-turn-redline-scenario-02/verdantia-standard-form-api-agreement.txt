Section 3.2 — Overage Notifications
Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to notify Licensee when Licensee's usage reaches [percentage threshold, e.g., 80]% of its Rate Limit allocation within a given measurement period. If Licensee exceeds its applicable Rate Limits during any measurement period, Provider may, at its sole option and discretion:
(a) throttle or temporarily suspend Licensee's access to the API Services until the commencement of the next measurement period, without liability to Licensee; or
(b) permit Licensee's continued access to the API Services beyond the applicable Rate Limits and charge Overage Fees as set forth in Schedule B for all API calls, queries, or transactions in excess of the applicable Rate Limits.
Overage Fees accrue automatically upon Licensee exceeding its Rate Limits and are invoiced in accordance with Section 4.2. Provider's election to permit continued access and charge Overage Fees in any given instance shall not constitute a waiver of Provider's right to throttle or suspend access in future instances, nor shall it create any obligation for Provider to permit continued access in the future.
Licensee may request an upgrade to a higher Usage Tier at any time by providing written notice to Provider. Upgraded Usage Tiers shall take effect upon Provider's written confirmation and corresponding adjustment of Fees. Licensee acknowledges that a downgrade to a lower Usage Tier during the Term may be subject to restrictions and may not result in a reduction of Fees already invoiced or paid.

ARTICLE 5 — SERVICE LEVELS AND CREDITS
Section 5.1 — Uptime Commitment
Provider shall maintain the API Services at or above the uptime percentage specified in Schedule C (the "Uptime Commitment"), measured on a [monthly/quarterly] basis. For purposes of this Agreement, "Uptime" means the percentage of time during the applicable measurement period that the API Services are available and functional for Licensee's authorized use, as determined by Provider's monitoring systems in accordance with the measurement methodology set forth in Schedule C.
The Uptime Commitment excludes from its calculation: (a) periods of Scheduled Maintenance performed in accordance with Section 5.2; (b) any unavailability caused by Force Majeure Events as defined in Section 15.7; (c) any unavailability resulting from Licensee's acts or omissions, including Licensee's misuse of the API Services, failure to comply with the Documentation, or use of non-supported configurations; (d) any unavailability resulting from failures or malfunctions of equipment, networks, or systems not within Provider's reasonable control; and (e) any unavailability resulting from Provider's suspension of Licensee's access in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.
Section 5.2 — Scheduled Maintenance
Provider shall provide at least [48/72] hours' prior written notice to Licensee of Scheduled Maintenance windows. Scheduled Maintenance shall, to the extent practicable, be performed during off-peak hours as specified in Schedule C. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to minimize the duration and frequency of Scheduled Maintenance and to avoid scheduling maintenance during Licensee's peak usage periods.
Scheduled Maintenance periods are excluded from Uptime calculations for purposes of the Uptime Commitment. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Provider reserves the right to perform emergency maintenance without prior notice if necessary to address critical security vulnerabilities, system failures, or other urgent circumstances that pose an imminent threat to the integrity, security, or availability of the API Services. Provider shall notify Licensee as soon as reasonably practicable of any emergency maintenance and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to minimize the duration and impact of such maintenance.
Section 5.3 — Service Level Credits
If Provider fails to meet the Uptime Commitment in any measurement period, Licensee shall be entitled to service credits ("SLA Credits") as set forth in Schedule C. SLA Credits are calculated as a percentage of the base subscription fee for the affected measurement period, based on the severity and duration of the downtime, in accordance with the SLA Credit schedule detailed in Schedule C.
SLA Credits represent Licensee's sole and exclusive remedy, and Provider's sole and exclusive liability, for any failure by Provider to meet the Uptime Commitment. The availability of SLA Credits is without prejudice to Licensee's right to terminate this Agreement for cause under Section 10.2 in the event of a material breach by Provider.
Section 5.4 — Credit Request Procedure
To receive SLA Credits, Licensee must submit a written request to Provider within thirty (30) days following the end of the measurement period in which the claimed failure to meet the Uptime Commitment occurred. The request must include: (a) a description of the downtime event(s) giving rise to the claim; (b) the dates and times of the claimed downtime; and (c) any available supporting documentation, such as error logs, screenshots, or monitoring data, substantiating the claimed downtime.
Provider shall review each SLA Credit request in good faith and shall respond to Licensee within fifteen (15) business days of receipt. If Provider confirms that the Uptime Commitment was not met during the applicable measurement period, Provider shall issue the applicable SLA Credits.
SLA Credits shall be applied as a credit against future invoices and are not redeemable for cash or refund. SLA Credits shall not exceed the maximum credit percentage specified in Schedule C (e.g., [25]%) of the base subscription fee for the applicable measurement period. SLA Credits may not be carried forward beyond twelve (12) months from the date of issuance and shall not be applied to Overage Fees, setup fees, or other non-subscription charges.
