Section 1 — Definitions
The following definitions apply to this Exhibit B. Unless otherwise specified, these definitions supplement and do not replace the definitions set forth in the Agreement.
"Availability" or "Uptime" means the percentage of time during a given calendar month (a Measurement Period) that the Verdantia Nexus API is operational and capable of receiving and responding to properly formatted API calls from Licensee, measured in accordance with the methodology described in Section 2 of this Exhibit B. For purposes of this definition, "operational" means that the Verdantia Nexus API production endpoints are accepting inbound requests and returning valid responses consistent with the then-current API documentation, without returning HTTP 5xx-series server error codes or connection timeout errors attributable to Verdantia's infrastructure.
"Downtime" means any period during which the Verdantia Nexus API is unavailable to Licensee, as determined by Verdantia's Monitoring System in accordance with Section 2.2, excluding any period that constitutes Excused Downtime. Downtime is measured in whole minutes; partial minutes at the beginning and end of a Downtime event shall each be rounded up to the nearest whole minute.
"Excused Downtime" means any period of unavailability of the Verdantia Nexus API that is attributable to one or more of the following causes:
(a) Scheduled Maintenance performed in accordance with Section 5 of this Exhibit B, including both regular Scheduled Maintenance conducted within permitted maintenance windows and the first two (2) hours of any emergency maintenance event;
(b) Force Majeure Events as defined in Section 12.4 of the Agreement, including without limitation natural disasters, acts of war or terrorism, pandemics, government orders, and other events beyond the reasonable control of Verdantia;
(c) failures, degradation, or latency in Licensee's own systems, networks, equipment, software, or internet connectivity, including failures in Licensee's firewall, proxy, or load-balancing configurations that prevent API calls from reaching Verdantia's infrastructure;
(d) Licensee's use of the Verdantia Nexus API in a manner that does not conform to the then-current API documentation published by Verdantia, including but not limited to the use of deprecated endpoints, improper authentication sequences, or request payloads that exceed documented size or rate limits;
(e) failures of the Domain Name System (DNS) or third-party infrastructure components that are outside of Verdantia's hosting environment and not within Verdantia's reasonable control, including outages at upstream internet backbone providers, content delivery networks not operated by Verdantia, or third-party certificate authorities; or
(f) any suspension of Licensee's access to the Verdantia Nexus API that is expressly permitted under the terms of the Agreement, including suspensions for non-payment, breach of acceptable use policies, or compliance with applicable law.
"Monthly Fee" means one-twelfth (1/12) of the Annual License Fee payable by Licensee under the Agreement, equaling One Hundred Five Thousand Dollars ($105,000) per month (calculated as $1,260,000 ÷ 12). The Monthly Fee is used solely as the basis for calculating SLA Credits under Section 4 and does not alter the invoicing schedule or payment terms set forth in the Agreement.
"Monitoring System" means Verdantia's automated infrastructure monitoring tools and associated software that continuously measure the availability, response time, and error rates of the Verdantia Nexus API from multiple geographic checkpoints distributed across at least three (3) distinct geographic regions within the continental United States. The Monitoring System operates independently of Licensee's systems and is maintained by Verdantia's site reliability engineering team.
"SLA Credit" means the service-level credit described in Section 4 of this Exhibit B, calculated as a percentage of the Monthly Fee and applied against future invoices in accordance with the procedures set forth therein.
"Scheduled Maintenance" means planned maintenance activities conducted by Verdantia in accordance with the notice requirements, timing restrictions, and other conditions set forth in Section 5 of this Exhibit B. Scheduled Maintenance includes, without limitation, software updates, hardware replacements, database migrations, security patching, and infrastructure upgrades that require temporary interruption of or degradation to Verdantia Nexus API services.
"Measurement Period" means each calendar month during the Term of the Agreement, beginning at 12:00:00 a.m. (Eastern Time) on the first day of such month and ending at 11:59:59 p.m. (Eastern Time) on the last day of such month. For the avoidance of doubt, the first Measurement Period shall commence on the Effective Date and end at 11:59:59 p.m. (Eastern Time) on August 31, 2025, and the final Measurement Period shall be the calendar month in which the Agreement expires or terminates, subject to the proration provisions of Section 2.4.
"Incident" means any unplanned event that results in Downtime or material degradation of the Verdantia Nexus API's performance below the parameters set forth in the then-current API documentation, including but not limited to complete service outages, elevated error rates, excessive response latency, data processing failures, or security events affecting the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of data transmitted through the API.
Section 2 — Uptime Measurement Methodology
2.1 — Calculation
Availability for each Measurement Period shall be calculated using the following formula:
Uptime % = ((Total Minutes in Measurement Period − Excused Downtime Minutes − Downtime Minutes) ÷ (Total Minutes in Measurement Period − Excused Downtime Minutes)) × 100
For purposes of this calculation, "Total Minutes in Measurement Period" means the total number of minutes in the applicable calendar month. By way of illustration, a thirty (30)-day month contains 43,200 total minutes, a thirty-one (31)-day month contains 44,640 total minutes, a twenty-eight (28)-day month contains 40,320 total minutes, and a twenty-nine (29)-day month contains 41,760 total minutes.
The numerator of the formula represents the total number of minutes during which the Verdantia Nexus API was operational and available to Licensee (i.e., total minutes less both Excused Downtime and Downtime). The denominator represents the total number of minutes against which Availability is measured (i.e., total minutes less Excused Downtime). This methodology ensures that periods of Excused Downtime are excluded from both the available time and the total measurement base, so that Excused Downtime has a neutral effect on the Uptime percentage calculation.
2.2 — Monitoring
Uptime shall be determined solely by reference to the data collected by Verdantia's Monitoring System. The parties agree that the Monitoring System shall serve as the authoritative source for Availability calculations and Downtime determinations, subject to the audit provisions set forth in Section 8.4.
The Monitoring System polls the Verdantia Nexus API production endpoints at intervals of no greater than sixty (60) seconds from each geographic checkpoint. A polling check consists of one or more synthetic API requests designed to test the end-to-end availability and responsiveness of the Verdantia Nexus API. A polling check is deemed to have "failed" if the API does not return a valid HTTP response (i.e., an HTTP 2xx-series response code) within the timeout threshold configured in the Monitoring System, or if the API returns an HTTP 5xx-series error code.
A polling failure from a majority of geographic checkpoints sustained for two (2) or more consecutive polling intervals shall constitute the beginning of a Downtime event. The requirement that failures be observed from a majority of geographic checkpoints ensures that localized network issues or transient connectivity anomalies at a single checkpoint do not trigger a Downtime event. The Downtime event shall be deemed to have commenced at the timestamp of the first failed polling interval in the sequence of consecutive failures.
The Downtime event concludes when the Monitoring System records a successful response from a majority of geographic checkpoints for two (2) consecutive polling intervals. The Downtime event shall be deemed to have ended at the timestamp of the second consecutive successful polling response.
2.3 — Rounding
Uptime percentages shall be calculated to three (3) decimal places and rounded to two (2) decimal places using standard rounding conventions (i.e., values of five-thousandths or greater shall be rounded up, and values below five-thousandths shall be rounded down). By way of example, an Uptime percentage calculated at 99.894% would be rounded to 99.89%, and an Uptime percentage calculated at 99.895% would be rounded to 99.90%.
2.4 — Partial Months
If the Effective Date or any termination or expiration date falls within a Measurement Period such that the Agreement is not in effect for the entirety of such calendar month, Availability shall be measured only for the portion of such month during which the Agreement is in effect. For purposes of any SLA Credit calculation applicable to a partial Measurement Period, the Monthly Fee shall be prorated on a per-diem basis (i.e., the Monthly Fee multiplied by the number of days the Agreement is in effect during the month, divided by the total number of days in such month). Total Minutes in Measurement Period shall likewise be adjusted to reflect only those minutes during which the Agreement was in effect.
Section 3 — Uptime Commitment
3.1 — Service Level Objective
Verdantia shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain Availability of the Verdantia Nexus API at or above ninety-nine and nine-tenths percent (99.9%) during each Measurement Period (the "Uptime Commitment"). The Uptime Commitment applies separately and independently to each Measurement Period; Availability achieved in prior or subsequent Measurement Periods shall have no bearing on the determination of whether the Uptime Commitment has been met for any particular Measurement Period.
For illustrative purposes, an Uptime Commitment of 99.9% equates to a maximum of approximately forty-three (43) minutes and twelve (12) seconds of permitted Downtime per thirty (30)-day Measurement Period (calculated as 43,200 total minutes × 0.001 = 43.2 minutes). Actual permitted Downtime will vary based on the number of days in the applicable Measurement Period and the amount of Excused Downtime occurring during such period.
The parties acknowledge that the Uptime Commitment represents a target that Verdantia commits to pursue with commercially reasonable efforts, and that the SLA Credits set forth in Section 4 constitute the agreed-upon remedy for failures to achieve the Uptime Commitment, subject to the limitations set forth herein.
3.2 — Measurement Exclusions
The Uptime Commitment shall not apply during any period of Excused Downtime, as defined in Section 1 of this Exhibit B. In no event shall Excused Downtime be counted as Downtime for purposes of determining whether Verdantia has met the Uptime Commitment during any Measurement Period. The burden of demonstrating that a particular period of unavailability constitutes Excused Downtime shall rest with Verdantia, which shall provide Licensee with reasonably detailed documentation supporting any such determination upon request.
Section 4 — SLA Credits
4.1 — Credit Calculation
If Verdantia fails to meet the Uptime Commitment during any Measurement Period, Licensee shall be entitled to SLA Credits calculated as set forth in this Section 4.1. SLA Credits accrue incrementally for each one-tenth of one percent (0.1%) by which actual Availability falls below the 99.9% Uptime Commitment during the applicable Measurement Period.
The credit rate shall be determined according to the following schedule:
Uptime Achieved During Measurement Period
SLA Credit Rate
99.80% – 99.89%
5% of the Monthly Fee per 0.1% shortfall below 99.9%
99.50% – 99.79%
5% of the Monthly Fee per 0.1% shortfall below 99.9%
Below 99.50%
5% of the Monthly Fee per 0.1% shortfall below 99.9%
For the avoidance of doubt, the credit accrues incrementally for each one-tenth of one percent (0.1%) by which actual Availability falls below the 99.9% Uptime Commitment. Shortfall increments shall be calculated based on the rounded Uptime percentage determined in accordance with Section 2.3.
Illustrative Example: If Availability in a Measurement Period is calculated at 99.70%, the shortfall below the 99.9% Uptime Commitment is 0.20%, representing two (2) increments of 0.1% each. The SLA Credit for that Measurement Period would be calculated as follows:
2 increments × 5% per increment × $105,000 Monthly Fee = $10,500
Additional Illustrative Example: If Availability in a Measurement Period is calculated at 99.30%, the shortfall below the 99.9% Uptime Commitment is 0.60%, representing six (6) increments of 0.1% each. The SLA Credit calculation before application of the monthly cap would be:
6 increments × 5% per increment × $105,000 Monthly Fee = $31,500
This amount equals the monthly cap described in Section 4.2 and would therefore be the maximum credit issued for that Measurement Period.
4.2 — Monthly Credit Cap
Notwithstanding Section 4.1, the aggregate SLA Credits for any single Measurement Period shall not exceed thirty percent (30%) of the Monthly Fee. Based on the current Monthly Fee of $105,000, the maximum SLA Credit for any single Measurement Period is Thirty-One Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($31,500). This cap applies regardless of the actual Uptime percentage achieved during the Measurement Period. If the Monthly Fee is adjusted during the Term in accordance with the Agreement, the monthly credit cap shall be recalculated based on the adjusted Monthly Fee.
4.3 — Credit Request Process
(a) Submission of Credit Request. To receive an SLA Credit, Licensee must submit a written request to Verdantia within thirty (30) calendar days after the end of the Measurement Period in which the Uptime Commitment was not met. Credit requests submitted after the expiration of this thirty (30)-day period shall be deemed waived and Licensee shall have no further right to claim SLA Credits with respect to such Measurement Period. Credit requests shall be submitted via email to the designated Verdantia account management contact or through the support portal, and must identify with reasonable specificity: (i) the Measurement Period at issue; (ii) the dates and approximate times (in Eastern Time) of each Downtime event observed by Licensee; (iii) a reasonable description of the impact of each Downtime event on Licensee's use of the Verdantia Nexus API; and (iv) Licensee's calculation of the requested SLA Credit amount.
(b) Review and Determination. Verdantia shall review each credit request in good faith and, within fifteen (15) business days of receipt of a complete credit request, shall either: (i) confirm the SLA Credit amount and notify Licensee in writing that such credit will be applied to the next quarterly invoice issued under the Agreement; or (ii) provide Licensee with a reasonably detailed written explanation of why Verdantia believes the Uptime Commitment was met during the applicable Measurement Period or why the claimed Downtime constitutes Excused Downtime, together with supporting data from the Monitoring System, including relevant logs and timestamps.
(c) Dispute Resolution. If the parties disagree as to whether an SLA Credit is owed or as to the amount of such credit, Verdantia shall provide Licensee with reasonable access to relevant Monitoring System logs and data for the Measurement Period in question, subject to the confidentiality obligations set forth in the Agreement. The parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any such dispute within fifteen (15) business days following Verdantia's delivery of its written explanation under subsection (b) above. If the parties are unable to resolve the dispute within such period, the dispute shall be subject to the dispute resolution provisions set forth in the Agreement, including any mandatory mediation or arbitration procedures specified therein.
4.4 — Form of Credit
SLA Credits shall be applied as a credit against the next quarterly invoice issued to Licensee following Verdantia's confirmation of the credit amount. SLA Credits are not refundable for cash during the Term of the Agreement, are not transferable to any third party, and may not be applied to any fees other than the Annual License Fee invoiced under the Agreement, including without limitation professional services fees, implementation fees, or overage charges.
If the Agreement expires or terminates before a confirmed SLA Credit has been fully applied against outstanding invoices, Verdantia shall issue the remaining unapplied credit amount to Licensee as a cash refund within thirty (30) calendar days following the effective date of expiration or termination. Such refund shall be paid to Licensee by wire transfer to the bank account designated in Licensee's most recent payment instructions on file with Verdantia.
In no event shall SLA Credits be cumulated across Measurement Periods for purposes of exceeding the monthly credit cap set forth in Section 4.2 with respect to any single Measurement Period. Each Measurement Period's SLA Credit, if any, shall be calculated independently.
4.5 — Sole and Exclusive Remedy
The SLA Credits set forth in this Section 4 are Licensee's sole and exclusive remedy, and Verdantia's sole and exclusive liability, for any failure to meet the Uptime Commitment during any Measurement Period. Licensee acknowledges and agrees that the SLA Credit mechanism represents a fair and reasonable allocation of risk between the parties with respect to service availability and that Verdantia's liability for Uptime shortfalls is appropriately limited to the credit amounts described herein.
Nothing in this Section 4.5 shall be construed to limit or otherwise affect Licensee's right to terminate the Agreement for material breach in accordance with the termination provisions set forth in the Agreement, provided that any such termination right shall be subject to the cure period and notice requirements set forth therein. For purposes of the Agreement's termination provisions, a failure to meet the Uptime Commitment in any single Measurement Period shall not, by itself, constitute a material breach of the Agreement; however, repeated or persistent failures to meet the Uptime Commitment over three (3) or more consecutive Measurement Periods, or over any five (5) Measurement Periods within a twelve (12)-month period, may be considered evidence of a material breach, subject to Verdantia's right to cure as provided in the Agreement.
Section 5 — Scheduled Maintenance
5.1 — Maintenance Window Allocation
Verdantia may perform Scheduled Maintenance for up to a maximum of four (4) hours per Measurement Period. Time consumed by Scheduled Maintenance that is properly noticed and conducted in accordance with the requirements of this Section 5 shall be treated as Excused Downtime and shall not count toward any Downtime calculation or affect the determination of whether the Uptime Commitment has been met for the applicable Measurement Period.
Verdantia shall use commercially reasonable efforts to minimize the duration and frequency of Scheduled Maintenance events and to complete maintenance activities within the shortest practicable timeframe. Verdantia shall further use commercially reasonable efforts to perform maintenance activities in a manner that minimizes disruption to Licensee's use of the Verdantia Nexus API, including by employing rolling deployment strategies, redundant infrastructure, and other techniques designed to reduce or eliminate service interruptions during maintenance windows where technically feasible.
5.2 — Notice Requirements
Verdantia shall provide Licensee with not less than seventy-two (72) hours prior written notice before commencing any Scheduled Maintenance event. Maintenance notices shall be delivered via email to Licensee's designated technical contact(s) identified pursuant to Section 7.3 of this Exhibit B, and shall simultaneously be posted to the Verdantia Nexus API status page maintained pursuant to Section 8.3.
Each maintenance notice shall include the following information:
(a) the date and estimated start time (in Eastern Time) of the maintenance event;
(b) the estimated duration of the maintenance event, expressed in hours and minutes;
(c) a general description of the maintenance activities to be performed, including a summary of the systems, components, or services affected; and
(d) the expected impact on Verdantia Nexus API availability during the maintenance event, including whether the API will be fully unavailable, partially available, or available with degraded performance.
In the event that a Scheduled Maintenance event is expected to exceed two (2) hours in duration, Verdantia shall provide Licensee with an additional reminder notice not less than twenty-four (24) hours prior to the scheduled start time.
5.3 — Permitted Maintenance Times
Scheduled Maintenance shall be performed during the following windows unless otherwise agreed in writing by the authorized representatives of both parties:
•  Primary Window: Saturday, 2:00 a.m. – 6:00 a.m. Eastern Time
•  Secondary Window: Sunday, 2:00 a.m. – 6:00 a.m. Eastern Time
Verdantia shall not perform Scheduled Maintenance outside of the Primary Window and Secondary Window without Licensee's prior written consent, except in the case of emergency maintenance as described below.
Emergency Maintenance. Notwithstanding the foregoing, emergency maintenance required to address critical security vulnerabilities, zero-day exploits, imminent system failures, or other conditions that pose an imminent risk of data loss, data corruption, security compromise, or extended outage may be performed outside the permitted maintenance windows, provided that Verdantia shall comply with each of the following requirements:
(i) Verdantia shall use commercially reasonable efforts to provide as much advance notice to Licensee as is practicable under the circumstances, taking into account the urgency and severity of the condition necessitating emergency maintenance;
(ii) Verdantia shall notify Licensee's designated technical contact by both telephone and email no later than simultaneously with the commencement of such emergency maintenance, providing a brief description of the condition necessitating the maintenance and the estimated duration;
(iii) Verdantia shall limit the duration of emergency maintenance to the minimum period reasonably necessary to address the condition, and shall provide Licensee with a status update upon completion of the emergency maintenance; and
(iv) Emergency maintenance shall be treated as Excused Downtime only for the first two (2) hours per occurrence. Any time in excess of two (2) hours of emergency maintenance per occurrence shall be counted as Downtime for purposes of the Uptime calculation and the Uptime Commitment.
Verdantia shall document each emergency maintenance event and include a summary thereof in the next monthly Uptime report delivered pursuant to Section 8.1.
5.4 — Carryover
Unused Scheduled Maintenance hours in any Measurement Period shall not carry over to any subsequent Measurement Period. Each Measurement Period's four (4)-hour maintenance allocation is independent, and Verdantia may not accumulate unused maintenance hours across Measurement Periods or draw upon future Measurement Periods' allocations to perform maintenance in the current period.
Section 6 — Incident Classification and Response
6.1 — Severity Levels
Incidents shall be classified according to the following severity levels, which determine the applicable response targets, resolution targets, and communication cadences. The severity levels reflect the relative business impact and urgency of each Incident category.
Priority 1 (P1) — Critical
A P1 Incident involves the complete unavailability of the Verdantia Nexus API production environment, rendering Licensee unable to submit or receive any API calls, or a confirmed security breach affecting the confidentiality or integrity of Licensee data transmitted through the API. No reasonable workaround is available to restore Licensee's use of the affected functionality.
•  Response Target: Initial acknowledgment and assignment of dedicated engineering resources within sixty (60) minutes of detection by the Monitoring System or Licensee's report of the Incident, whichever occurs earlier. Response shall include confirmation of receipt, the name and contact information of the assigned engineer, and an initial assessment of the issue.
•  Resolution Target: Restoration of service or implementation of a viable workaround that restores Licensee's material functionality within four (4) hours of initial response. A "viable workaround" means an alternative means of accessing the affected API functionality that, while not constituting a permanent fix, enables Licensee to resume core production operations.
•  Communication Cadence: Status updates shall be provided to Licensee's designated technical and business contacts every thirty (30) minutes from the time of initial response until the Incident is resolved or a viable workaround is implemented. Updates shall include the current status of the investigation, actions being taken, and the estimated time to resolution.
Priority 2 (P2) — High
A P2 Incident involves material degradation of Verdantia Nexus API performance that affects Licensee's production operations but does not constitute a complete outage. Examples include error rates exceeding five percent (5%) of API calls over a sustained period, average response latency exceeding two times (2×) the documented performance thresholds, or the unavailability of one or more critical API endpoints while other endpoints remain operational. A workaround may or may not be available.
•  Response Target: Initial acknowledgment within two (2) hours of detection by the Monitoring System or Licensee's report of the Incident, whichever occurs earlier. Response shall include confirmation of receipt and assignment of responsible personnel.
•  Resolution Target: Restoration of normal performance levels consistent with the API documentation within eight (8) hours of initial response. If a permanent fix cannot be implemented within this timeframe, Verdantia shall implement a workaround and provide Licensee with a timeline for delivery of a permanent resolution.
•  Communication Cadence: Status updates shall be provided to Licensee's designated technical and business contacts every sixty (60) minutes from the time of initial response until the Incident is resolved or a viable workaround is implemented.
Priority 3 (P3) — Medium
A P3 Incident involves partial impairment of non-critical Verdantia Nexus API functionality, or intermittent errors that do not materially affect Licensee's core production workflows. A reasonable workaround is available that enables Licensee to continue normal operations with minimal disruption. Examples include intermittent timeout errors on non-critical endpoints, degraded performance on secondary API functions, or minor data formatting inconsistencies.
•  Response Target: Initial acknowledgment within four (4) business hours of Licensee's report of the Incident. Response shall include confirmation of receipt and a preliminary assessment of the issue.
•  Resolution Target: Remediation of the underlying issue within two (2) business days of initial response. If remediation requires a longer timeframe, Verdantia shall provide Licensee with a remediation plan and estimated completion date.
•  Communication Cadence: Status updates shall be provided to Licensee's designated technical contact every four (4) business hours until the Incident is resolved.
Priority 4 (P4) — Low
A P4 Incident involves minor issues, cosmetic defects, documentation errors, or feature requests that do not impact the functionality, performance, or availability of the Verdantia Nexus API. Examples include typographical errors in API response messages, requests for additional documentation, suggestions for API enhancements, or minor user interface issues in the developer portal.
•  Response Target: Initial acknowledgment within one (1) business day of Licensee's report of the issue or request.
•  Resolution Target: Addressed in the next scheduled maintenance release, or as otherwise mutually agreed by the parties in writing. Verdantia shall include P4 items in its internal product backlog and shall provide Licensee with visibility into the prioritization of such items upon request.
•  Communication Cadence: Updates shall be provided upon Licensee's request or included in release notes accompanying the maintenance release in which the item is addressed.
6.2 — Severity Classification Authority
Verdantia shall make the initial severity classification for each Incident based on the information available at the time of detection or initial report. Verdantia's initial classification shall be communicated to Licensee as part of the initial response.
Licensee may request reclassification of an Incident to a higher severity level by providing a written description of the business impact experienced by Licensee, including specific details regarding the nature of the disruption, the number of affected users or systems, and the operational or financial consequences of the Incident. Such reclassification requests may be submitted via the support portal, email, or telephone (for P1 reclassification requests).
Verdantia shall consider each reclassification request in good faith, giving due weight to Licensee's description of business impact, and shall respond to such request within the following timeframes:
•  Requests to reclassify to P1: Response within thirty (30) minutes of receipt of the reclassification request.
•  All other reclassification requests: Response within two (2) hours of receipt of the reclassification request.
If the parties cannot agree on the appropriate severity classification for an Incident, the Incident shall be treated at the higher severity level proposed by either party pending resolution of the disagreement. The parties shall escalate the classification dispute to the applicable contacts identified in the escalation path set forth in Section 7.2 for prompt resolution.
6.3 — Response and Resolution Measurement
(a) Definition of "Response." For purposes of this Exhibit B, "Response" means the initial acknowledgment by a qualified Verdantia support engineer, communicated to Licensee via the designated support channel (the support ticket system for P2 through P4 Incidents, and the support ticket system supplemented by telephone and/or email for P1 Incidents), confirming receipt of the Incident report and assignment of responsible personnel. An automated ticket confirmation generated by the support portal does not constitute a "Response" for purposes of this Section 6.3; a human acknowledgment from a qualified engineer is required.
(b) Definition of "Resolution." For purposes of this Exhibit B, "Resolution" means restoration of the Verdantia Nexus API to operational status consistent with the Uptime Commitment and the performance parameters set forth in the API documentation, or delivery of a reasonable workaround that restores Licensee's material functionality pending implementation of a permanent fix. A permanent fix may be delivered in a subsequent maintenance release, provided that Verdantia communicates the timeline for the permanent fix to Licensee and such timeline is commercially reasonable under the circumstances.
(c) Measurement Hours. Response and resolution times for P3 and P4 Incidents are measured exclusively during business hours, defined as Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time, excluding federal holidays observed by Verdantia. Response and resolution times for P1 and P2 Incidents are measured on a twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week, three hundred sixty-five days per year (24×7×365) basis, without regard to business hours, weekends, or holidays.

7.4 — Post-Incident Review
For each P1 Incident and, upon Licensee's reasonable request, each P2 Incident, Verdantia shall prepare and deliver to Licensee a written post-incident report (each, a "Root Cause Analysis" or "RCA") within five (5) business days following the resolution of the Incident. Verdantia shall deliver the RCA via email to Licensee's designated technical and business contacts, and shall upload a copy to the support portal for Licensee's records.
Each RCA shall include, at a minimum, the following elements:
(a) Incident Timeline: A detailed chronological timeline of the Incident from the time of initial detection or report through final resolution, including key milestones such as escalation triggers, diagnostic findings, and remedial actions taken at each stage.
(b) Root Cause Identification: A thorough analysis of the underlying root cause(s) of the Incident, including identification of the specific systems, components, configurations, code changes, or external factors that contributed to the failure or degradation.
(c) Impact Assessment: A quantitative and qualitative assessment of the Incident's impact, including the total duration of Downtime (in minutes), the API endpoints or services affected, the estimated number of failed or degraded API calls during the Incident period, and any effects on the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of Licensee data.
(d) Remedial Actions: A description of the immediate remedial actions taken to resolve the Incident and restore service, including any temporary workarounds deployed.
(e) Preventive Measures: A description of the long-term preventive measures that Verdantia will implement to reduce the likelihood of recurrence of the same or similar Incidents, including timelines for implementation of such measures where applicable.
Verdantia and Licensee shall, upon either party's reasonable request, conduct a joint review call within ten (10) business days of delivery of the RCA to discuss the findings, answer questions, and align on preventive measures. The joint review call shall include appropriate technical and management representatives from both parties.

(b) Total Downtime minutes and total Excused Downtime minutes incurred during the Measurement Period, with a detailed breakdown of each individual Downtime event and each individual Excused Downtime event, including the date, start time (Eastern Time), end time (Eastern Time), total duration (in minutes), and a brief narrative description of the cause of each event.

(d) A summary of all Incidents classified as P1 or P2 during the Measurement Period, including for each such Incident: the severity level assigned, the date and time of initial report or detection, the actual response time, the actual resolution time, the root cause (if determined), and the current status (resolved, workaround in place, or ongoing).
(e) The cumulative year-to-date Uptime percentage, calculated as the weighted average of Uptime percentages across all Measurement Periods elapsed since the beginning of the current calendar year (or, if the Agreement commenced during the current calendar year, since the Effective Date).
