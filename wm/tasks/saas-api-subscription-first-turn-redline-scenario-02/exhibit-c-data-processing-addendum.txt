EXHIBIT C
DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM
to the API Access and Use Agreement (Ref. VER-API-2025-0047)
between
Verdantia Systems Inc.
and
Hollowell Industries Group LLC
Effective Date: August 1, 2025
Section 1 — Purpose and Scope
1.1 This Data Processing Addendum (this "DPA") is incorporated into and forms an integral part of the API Access and Use Agreement dated August 1, 2025 (the "Agreement"), entered into between Verdantia Systems Inc. ("Verdantia"), a Delaware C-Corporation with its principal office at 440 Crescent Ridge Boulevard, Suite 800, Wilmington, DE 19801, and Hollowell Industries Group LLC ("Hollowell"), a Delaware limited liability company with its principal office at 70 Gramercy Park South, 14th Floor, New York, NY 10003. Unless the context otherwise requires, the terms "Verdantia" and "Hollowell" shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Agreement.
1.2 This DPA governs the Processing of Personal Data transmitted through, generated by, or otherwise associated with the Verdantia Nexus API in connection with Hollowell's use of the Verdantia Nexus API and its integration with Hollowell's proprietary supply chain management platform ("Hollowell SCM"). The parties acknowledge that the performance of the Agreement necessarily involves the Processing of Personal Data and that this DPA is intended to ensure that such Processing is conducted in compliance with Applicable Data Protection Laws, as defined herein.
1.3 In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between the terms of this DPA and the terms of the Agreement (including any schedules, exhibits, or appendices thereto other than this DPA), the terms of this DPA shall prevail to the extent that such conflict or inconsistency relates to the Processing of Personal Data. In all other respects, the terms of the Agreement shall govern the relationship between the parties.
1.4 This DPA applies to all Personal Data Processed by Verdantia on behalf of Hollowell under or in connection with the Agreement, regardless of the country or jurisdiction in which the Data Subject resides, is located, or holds citizenship. The obligations set forth in this DPA are intended to be interpreted and applied in a manner consistent with the highest applicable standard of data protection required by Applicable Data Protection Laws in any relevant jurisdiction.
Section 2 — Definitions
For purposes of this DPA, the following capitalized terms shall have the meanings set forth below. All other capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA shall have the meanings assigned to them in the Agreement.
2.1 "Applicable Data Protection Laws" means the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 ("GDPR"); the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (Cal. Civ. Code §§ 1798.100–1798.199.100) ("CCPA"); and any other applicable data protection or privacy law of a U.S. state, including but not limited to the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act (Va. Code Ann. §§ 59.1-575 et seq.), the Colorado Privacy Act (Colo. Rev. Stat. §§ 6-1-1301 et seq.), and the Connecticut Data Privacy Act (Conn. Gen. Stat. §§ 42-515 et seq.), in each case as amended, supplemented, re-enacted, or replaced from time to time.
2.2 "Controller" means the entity that determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data. As between the parties to this DPA, Hollowell is the Controller with respect to the Personal Data Processed under the Agreement.
2.3 "Data Breach" means any accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Personal Data transmitted, stored, or otherwise Processed by Verdantia or its Sub-processors under or in connection with this DPA, regardless of the medium on which such Personal Data is stored or the means by which such Personal Data is accessed.
2.4 "Data Subject" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates. An identifiable natural person is one who can be identified, directly or indirectly, in particular by reference to an identifier such as a name, an identification number, location data, an online identifier, or one or more factors specific to the physical, physiological, genetic, mental, economic, cultural, or social identity of that natural person.
2.5 "Personal Data" means any information relating to a Data Subject that is Processed by Verdantia on behalf of Hollowell in connection with the Agreement. For the avoidance of doubt, Personal Data includes "personal information" as defined under the CCPA and analogous terms under other Applicable Data Protection Laws.
2.6 "Processing" (and its cognate terms "Process," "Processed," and "Processes") means any operation or set of operations that is performed on Personal Data or on sets of Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, including without limitation collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination or otherwise making available, alignment or combination, restriction, erasure, or destruction.
2.7 "Processor" means the entity that Processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller. As between the parties to this DPA, Verdantia is the Processor with respect to the Personal Data Processed under the Agreement.
2.8 "Standard Contractual Clauses" or "SCCs" means the standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to processors established in third countries, as adopted by the European Commission pursuant to Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021 on standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council, as such clauses may be amended, superseded, or replaced from time to time by the European Commission or any competent authority.
2.9 "Sub-processor" means any third party (other than Verdantia's employees) engaged by Verdantia, or by any existing Sub-processor of Verdantia, to Process Personal Data on behalf of Hollowell in connection with the performance of Verdantia's obligations under this DPA and the Agreement.
2.10 "Supervisory Authority" means an independent public authority established by an EU/EEA Member State pursuant to Article 51 of the GDPR, or any analogous regulatory or supervisory authority with competence over data protection matters under Applicable Data Protection Laws, including the California Privacy Protection Agency, the Virginia Attorney General, and any other U.S. state attorney general or regulatory body with enforcement authority under applicable U.S. state privacy laws.
2.11 Incorporation by Reference. All capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined in this DPA shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Agreement.
Section 3 — Roles and Responsibilities
3.1 Controller and Processor Designation. The parties acknowledge and agree that, with respect to the Personal Data Processed under this DPA and the Agreement, Hollowell acts as the Controller and Verdantia acts as the Processor. Nothing in this DPA shall be construed to establish any joint controller relationship between the parties, and each party agrees that it shall not Process Personal Data in a manner that would create such a relationship without the prior written consent of the other party.
3.2 Processing on Instructions. Verdantia shall Process Personal Data only on and in accordance with documented instructions from Hollowell, unless required to do so by applicable law to which Verdantia is subject (including, without limitation, applicable law of the European Union, an EU/EEA Member State, or any applicable U.S. federal or state law). In the event that Verdantia is required by applicable law to Process Personal Data other than in accordance with Hollowell's documented instructions, Verdantia shall inform Hollowell of that legal requirement before carrying out the relevant Processing, unless such law prohibits Verdantia from providing such information on important grounds of public interest. The documented instructions of Hollowell as of the Effective Date are set forth in this DPA, the Agreement, and the applicable schedules and exhibits thereto.
3.3 Hollowell's Responsibility for Lawfulness. Hollowell retains sole responsibility for determining the lawfulness of the Processing it instructs Verdantia to perform, including the establishment and maintenance of a lawful basis for Processing under Applicable Data Protection Laws (including, where applicable, obtaining any necessary consents from Data Subjects or ensuring the applicability of another lawful basis under Article 6 of the GDPR or analogous provisions of other Applicable Data Protection Laws). Verdantia shall have no liability arising from Hollowell's failure to establish or maintain a lawful basis for Processing.
3.4 Notification of Unlawful Instructions. Where Verdantia reasonably believes that an instruction received from Hollowell infringes Applicable Data Protection Laws, Verdantia shall promptly notify Hollowell in writing of the basis for its belief. Upon delivering such notice, Verdantia may suspend the relevant Processing activity pending receipt of revised instructions from Hollowell that Verdantia reasonably determines to be compliant with Applicable Data Protection Laws. Such suspension shall not constitute a breach of the Agreement or this DPA, and Verdantia shall not be liable for any delay or non-performance attributable to such suspension.
Section 4 — Categories of Data Subjects and Personal Data
Section 4.1 — Data Subjects
The Personal Data Processed under this DPA may relate to the following categories of Data Subjects:
(a) Employees, Contractors, and Authorized End Users of Hollowell. Individuals employed by or engaged as independent contractors of Hollowell, as well as authorized end users, who access or interact with the Verdantia Nexus API through the Hollowell SCM platform or related interfaces.
(b) Hollowell's Customers and End Customers. Individuals who are customers, prospective customers, or end customers of Hollowell, whose Personal Data transits the Verdantia Nexus API in connection with supply chain management operations, including order processing, shipment tracking, delivery coordination, and related logistical activities.
(c) Representatives of Suppliers, Vendors, and Logistics Partners. Representatives, contact persons, and other individuals associated with Hollowell's suppliers, vendors, freight carriers, customs brokers, logistics partners, and other third parties whose Personal Data is transmitted through or Processed by the Verdantia Nexus API in the course of Hollowell's supply chain operations.
Section 4.2 — Categories of Personal Data
The following categories of Personal Data may be Processed by Verdantia under this DPA:
(a) Identification Data. Full names, employee identification numbers, user identification numbers, email addresses, telephone numbers, business mailing addresses, and job titles.
(b) Authentication and Access Credentials. API tokens, session identifiers, hashed or encrypted passwords (to the extent such credentials are transmitted via or stored in connection with the Verdantia Nexus API), and multi-factor authentication metadata.
(c) Transactional Data. Purchase order information, invoice data, shipment records, delivery addresses, bill of lading data, customs documentation references, and other supply chain transaction data that contains personal identifiers of the Data Subjects described in Section 4.1.
(d) Technical Data. IP addresses, device identifiers, browser type and version, API call logs, request and response metadata, timestamps, error logs, and geolocation data (where the geolocation feature is enabled by Hollowell or its authorized end users).
4.2.1 Special Categories of Personal Data. No special categories of Personal Data (as defined in Article 9 of the GDPR), including data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data for the purpose of uniquely identifying a natural person, data concerning health, or data concerning a natural person's sex life or sexual orientation, are intended to be Processed under this DPA. Hollowell shall not transmit or cause to be transmitted any such special categories of Personal Data through the Verdantia Nexus API without the prior written agreement of Verdantia and the implementation of supplementary technical and organizational safeguards mutually agreed upon by the parties.
Section 5 — Processing Purposes and Instructions
5.1 Permitted Purposes. Verdantia shall Process Personal Data solely for the following purposes, and shall not Process Personal Data for any purpose other than those set forth below unless expressly authorized in writing by Hollowell:
(a) Providing, operating, maintaining, and supporting the Verdantia Nexus API in accordance with the terms of the Agreement and the applicable Service Levels set forth in Schedule B to the Agreement, including processing API requests, delivering API responses, and maintaining the API infrastructure necessary for Hollowell's use of the services;
(b) Performing API call routing, load balancing, caching, rate limiting, and error handling operations necessary for the reliable and efficient operation of the Verdantia Nexus API;
(c) Monitoring system performance, availability, uptime, latency, throughput, and security of the Verdantia Nexus API, including incident detection, diagnostics, and resolution activities;
(d) Generating anonymized, aggregated analytics and usage statistics for the purpose of improving the Verdantia Nexus API platform, subject to and in accordance with Section 6 of the Agreement regarding Derived Data, provided that such anonymization is performed using industry-standard techniques that render the data no longer reasonably capable of being linked to or associated with any identifiable Data Subject;
(e) Complying with Applicable Data Protection Laws and other applicable legal obligations, including responding to Data Subject requests as set forth in Section 9 of this DPA; and
(f) Any other purpose expressly authorized in a written instrument signed by an authorized representative of Hollowell.
5.2 Documented Instructions. Hollowell's written instructions for Processing are set forth in this DPA, the Agreement (including all exhibits, schedules, and appendices thereto), and any subsequent written instructions mutually agreed upon by the parties in accordance with the change management procedures set forth in the Agreement. Oral instructions shall not constitute valid Processing instructions for purposes of this DPA, and Verdantia shall not be required to act upon any instruction that is not provided in writing (which, for purposes of this Section, includes email correspondence from Hollowell's designated contact).
Section 6 — Confidentiality
6.1 Personnel Obligations. Verdantia shall ensure that all personnel (including employees, temporary workers, and individual contractors) who are authorized to Process Personal Data under this DPA are bound by written obligations of confidentiality with respect to such Personal Data, whether arising under a confidentiality agreement, employment contract, or statutory obligation of professional secrecy. Such confidentiality obligations shall survive the termination of the individual's engagement with Verdantia and shall prohibit the unauthorized disclosure, use, or access to Personal Data.
6.2 Access Limitations. Verdantia shall ensure that access to Personal Data is restricted on a need-to-know basis and is limited to those personnel who require such access to perform Verdantia's obligations under the Agreement and this DPA. Verdantia shall maintain and enforce access control policies and procedures to prevent unauthorized access to Personal Data by personnel who do not have a legitimate business need for such access.
Section 7 — Security Measures
Section 7.1 — Technical and Organizational Measures
7.1.1 Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of Processing, as well as the risk of varying likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of Data Subjects, Verdantia shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk of Processing. Such measures shall include, at a minimum and as applicable:
(a) Encryption. Encryption of Personal Data in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS) version 1.2 or higher, and encryption of Personal Data at rest using the Advanced Encryption Standard with a key length of 256 bits (AES-256) or an equivalent or stronger encryption standard. Verdantia shall maintain key management procedures that ensure the secure generation, storage, rotation, and destruction of encryption keys.
(b) Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, and Resilience. Measures designed to ensure the ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of the systems and services used to Process Personal Data, including redundant infrastructure, failover capabilities, and disaster recovery planning.
(c) Data Recovery. The ability to restore the availability of and access to Personal Data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident, including the maintenance of regular backups and documented recovery procedures with defined recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs).
(d) Multi-Factor Authentication. Multi-factor authentication for all administrative access to systems, platforms, databases, and infrastructure components that are used to Process Personal Data, ensuring that no single-factor credential is sufficient to obtain privileged access.
(e) Network Security. Network segmentation to isolate systems Processing Personal Data from other network environments, deployment of intrusion detection and prevention systems (IDS/IPS), and regular vulnerability scanning and penetration testing conducted at least annually (and promptly following any material change to the network environment) by qualified internal or external security professionals.
(f) Access Controls. Role-based access controls (RBAC) that enforce the principle of least privilege, coupled with regular access reviews conducted no less frequently than quarterly to verify that access rights are appropriate and that terminated or reassigned personnel have had their access promptly revoked.
(g) Testing and Evaluation. A documented process for regularly testing, assessing, and evaluating the effectiveness of the technical and organizational measures described in this Section 7.1, including internal security audits, tabletop exercises, and remediation tracking.
7.1.2 Verdantia shall periodically review and update the technical and organizational measures described in this Section 7.1 to reflect changes in the threat landscape, evolving best practices, and changes in Applicable Data Protection Laws. Any material reduction in the level of security provided by such measures shall require Hollowell's prior written consent.
Section 7.2 — SOC 2 Type II Certification
7.2.1 Verdantia shall obtain and maintain a current SOC 2 Type II report prepared in accordance with the AICPA Trust Services Criteria, audited by Clairemont & Sadler CPAs LLP (or a successor firm of comparable standing and reputation approved by Hollowell, such approval not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed). The SOC 2 Type II report shall cover, at a minimum, the trust service criteria of security, availability, and confidentiality as they relate to the systems and services used to Process Personal Data under this DPA.
7.2.2 Verdantia shall provide a copy of its most recent SOC 2 Type II report to Hollowell within thirty (30) days of Hollowell's written request, provided that Hollowell shall not make more than one (1) such request in any twelve-month period. Provision of the SOC 2 Type II report shall be subject to Hollowell's execution of Verdantia's standard non-disclosure agreement for audit reports (or, where a mutual non-disclosure agreement is already in effect between the parties that covers such reports, such existing agreement shall apply).
Section 8 — Sub-processors
Section 8.1 — General Authorization
8.1.1 Hollowell hereby grants Verdantia a general written authorization to engage Sub-processors to Process Personal Data in connection with the performance of Verdantia's obligations under the Agreement and this DPA, subject to the requirements set forth in this Section 8. This general authorization does not relieve Verdantia of any of its obligations under this DPA with respect to the Processing of Personal Data by any Sub-processor.
8.1.2 Verdantia shall establish and maintain a current list of Sub-processors, which shall include each Sub-processor's name, a description of the Processing activities performed by such Sub-processor, and the location(s) where the Sub-processor Processes Personal Data. Verdantia shall make such list available to Hollowell upon written request and shall publish an up-to-date version of such list at a URL accessible to Hollowell (the "Sub-processor List URL"), which URL shall be communicated to Hollowell upon execution of this DPA and promptly following any change thereto.
Section 8.2 — Notification of New Sub-processors
8.2.1 Verdantia shall notify Hollowell in writing at least thirty (30) days prior to engaging any new Sub-processor or replacing an existing Sub-processor (each, a "Sub-processor Change"). Such notification shall include the identity of the proposed Sub-processor, the nature and scope of the Processing to be performed by the proposed Sub-processor, and the location(s) where the Processing will occur.
8.2.2 Hollowell shall have a period of twenty (20) business days from the date of receipt of such notice (the "Objection Window") to object to the proposed Sub-processor Change. Any objection must be made on reasonable data-protection grounds and must be communicated to Verdantia in writing, setting forth the specific basis for Hollowell's objection with sufficient detail to enable Verdantia to evaluate and respond to such objection.
8.2.3 If Hollowell raises a timely and valid objection in accordance with Section 8.2.2, the parties shall negotiate in good faith for a period not to exceed fifteen (15) business days following Verdantia's receipt of Hollowell's objection to resolve the objection. Such resolution may include, without limitation, Verdantia proposing an alternative Sub-processor, implementing supplementary technical or organizational safeguards, or modifying the scope or location of the Processing to be performed by the proposed Sub-processor. If the parties are unable to resolve the objection within such fifteen (15) business day period, Hollowell may, as its sole and exclusive remedy with respect to such Sub-processor Change, terminate the affected Processing activity (but not the Agreement as a whole) upon written notice to Verdantia, and Verdantia shall promptly cease the affected Processing and return or delete the relevant Personal Data in accordance with Section 15 of this DPA.
8.2.4 If Hollowell does not deliver a written objection to Verdantia within the Objection Window, Hollowell shall be deemed to have approved the proposed Sub-processor Change, and Verdantia shall be authorized to engage the proposed Sub-processor in accordance with the terms of this DPA.
Section 8.3 — Sub-processor Obligations
8.3.1 Verdantia shall enter into a written agreement with each Sub-processor prior to the Sub-processor's commencement of any Processing of Personal Data. Such agreement shall impose data protection obligations that are no less protective than those set forth in this DPA, including without limitation obligations regarding the confidentiality of Personal Data, the implementation and maintenance of appropriate technical and organizational security measures, cooperation with Data Subject rights requests, compliance with applicable audit requirements, and the obligation to Process Personal Data only in accordance with Hollowell's documented instructions as communicated through Verdantia.
8.3.2 Verdantia shall remain fully liable to Hollowell for the performance of each Sub-processor's obligations under the applicable Sub-processor agreement. Any failure by a Sub-processor to fulfill its data protection obligations under such agreement shall be treated, for all purposes of this DPA and the Agreement, as a failure by Verdantia to fulfill its corresponding obligations under this DPA.
Section 8.4 — Current Sub-processors
As of the Effective Date, Verdantia's list of authorized Sub-processors, including each Sub-processor's name, Processing activity, location, and date of engagement, shall be provided to Hollowell as Appendix 1 to this DPA. The list set forth in Appendix 1 shall be deemed to have been approved by Hollowell as of the Effective Date.
Section 9 — Data Subject Rights
Section 9.1 — Assistance with Requests
9.1.1 Verdantia shall, taking into account the nature of the Processing and the information available to Verdantia, assist Hollowell by implementing appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as this is reasonably possible, for the fulfillment of Hollowell's obligation to respond to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under Applicable Data Protection Laws. Such rights include, without limitation, the right of access, the right to rectification, the right to erasure (right to be forgotten), the right to restriction of Processing, the right to data portability, the right to object to Processing, and the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing (including profiling), in each case to the extent applicable under the relevant Applicable Data Protection Law.
9.1.2 Verdantia shall promptly, and in any event within five (5) business days, notify Hollowell if Verdantia receives a request directly from a Data Subject relating to Personal Data Processed under this DPA. Verdantia shall not respond to any such request directly unless expressly authorized in writing by Hollowell or required to do so by applicable law, in which case Verdantia shall, to the extent permitted by applicable law, inform Hollowell of that legal requirement prior to responding to the Data Subject's request.
Section 9.2 — Costs
Where Verdantia's assistance with Data Subject requests requires effort that is materially beyond the scope of Verdantia's obligations under the Agreement (including, without limitation, requests that require the development of custom tools, the manual review of large data sets, or the allocation of significant engineering resources), the parties shall agree in advance and in writing on reasonable compensation for such assistance, taking into account the nature and complexity of the request and the resources required.
Section 10 — Data Breach Notification
Section 10.1 — Notification Obligation
Verdantia shall notify Hollowell without undue delay, and in any event no later than seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of a Data Breach, by written notice directed to Hollowell's designated contact: Derek Calloway, Senior Corporate Counsel, Hollowell Industries Group LLC, 70 Gramercy Park South, 14th Floor, New York, NY 10003 (email and phone as specified in the Agreement), or such other person as Hollowell may designate in writing from time to time. Verdantia shall be deemed to have become "aware" of a Data Breach when any officer, director, or employee of Verdantia with responsibility for information security or data protection has a reasonable basis to believe that a Data Breach has occurred.
Section 10.2 — Content of Notification
10.2.1 The notification required under Section 10.1 shall include, to the extent the following information is reasonably available to Verdantia at the time of notification:
(a) A description of the nature of the Data Breach, including, where possible, the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects concerned and the categories and approximate number of Personal Data records concerned;
(b) The name and contact details of Verdantia's data protection point of contact or other designated contact person from whom Hollowell may obtain additional information;
(c) A description of the likely consequences of the Data Breach, including an assessment of the potential risk to the rights and freedoms of affected Data Subjects;
(d) A description of the measures taken or proposed to be taken by Verdantia to address the Data Breach, including, where appropriate, measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects and to prevent recurrence.
10.2.2 Where, and to the extent that, it is not possible to provide all of the information described in Section 10.2.1 simultaneously with the initial notification, Verdantia shall provide information in phases without undue further delay as such information becomes available. Verdantia shall document the facts relating to the Data Breach, its effects, and the remedial action taken, and shall provide such documentation to Hollowell upon request.
Section 10.3 — Cooperation
10.3.1 Verdantia shall cooperate with Hollowell and take such reasonable steps as Hollowell may direct to assist in the investigation, mitigation, and remediation of the Data Breach, including the preservation of evidence, the implementation of interim security measures, and cooperation with law enforcement authorities and Supervisory Authorities as may be necessary or appropriate.
10.3.2 Verdantia shall not issue any public statement, press release, notification to Data Subjects, or other public communication regarding a Data Breach involving Hollowell's Personal Data without Hollowell's prior written consent, except where Verdantia is required by applicable law to make such a communication, in which case Verdantia shall, to the extent permitted by applicable law, provide Hollowell with advance notice of the content and timing of such communication and shall reasonably consider any comments or requested modifications provided by Hollowell.
Section 11 — Data Protection Impact Assessments and Prior Consultation
Verdantia shall provide reasonable assistance to Hollowell, at Hollowell's request and expense, in conducting data protection impact assessments ("DPIAs") and, where required, prior consultations with Supervisory Authorities pursuant to Articles 35 and 36 of the GDPR or analogous provisions of other Applicable Data Protection Laws, in each case to the extent such DPIA or prior consultation is required under Applicable Data Protection Laws in connection with Hollowell's use of the Verdantia Nexus API and taking into account the nature of the Processing and the information reasonably available to Verdantia. Verdantia's obligation under this Section 11 includes providing information regarding the technical and organizational measures implemented by Verdantia, the nature of the Processing performed by Verdantia, and any other information reasonably required by Hollowell to complete a DPIA or engage in a prior consultation.
Section 12 — Cross-Border Data Transfers
Section 12.1 — Transfer Restrictions
12.1.1 Verdantia shall not transfer Personal Data to, or access Personal Data from, any country or territory outside of the European Economic Area ("EEA"), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland (collectively, "Restricted Transfers") unless an adequate level of data protection is ensured by one or more of the following mechanisms:
(a) An adequacy decision adopted by the European Commission pursuant to Article 45 of the GDPR with respect to the recipient country or territory, including, where applicable, reliance on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (or any successor framework);
(b) Standard Contractual Clauses adopted pursuant to Article 46(2)(c) of the GDPR, as set forth in Annex A to this DPA;
(c) Binding Corporate Rules approved by a competent Supervisory Authority pursuant to Article 47 of the GDPR;
(d) Any other lawful transfer mechanism recognized under Applicable Data Protection Laws, including derogations under Article 49 of the GDPR where applicable and subject to the conditions and limitations set forth therein.
12.1.2 For the avoidance of doubt, any transfer of Personal Data to Sub-processors located outside the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland shall comply with the requirements of this Section 12 and shall be subject to the Sub-processor engagement requirements of Section 8.
Section 12.2 — Standard Contractual Clauses
12.2.1 To the extent that the transfer of Personal Data from Hollowell to Verdantia constitutes a Restricted Transfer, the parties agree that Module Two (Controller-to-Processor) of the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted pursuant to Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021 shall apply to such transfer, and such Standard Contractual Clauses are hereby incorporated into this DPA by reference. The parties agree to the following clause-specific selections:
(a) Clause 7 (Docking clause). The optional docking clause shall apply, permitting additional parties to accede to the Standard Contractual Clauses in the future in accordance with the terms set forth therein.
(b) Clause 9(a) (Use of sub-processors). Option 2 (General written authorisation) shall apply, consistent with the Sub-processor engagement provisions of Section 8 of this DPA. The Processor shall inform the Controller of any intended changes to the list of Sub-processors with a prior notice period of thirty (30) days, thereby giving the Controller sufficient time to object to such changes in accordance with Section 8.2 of this DPA.
(c) Clause 11 (Redress). The optional language regarding an independent dispute resolution body shall not apply.
(d) Clause 13 (Supervision) and Annex I.C (Competent Supervisory Authority). The competent Supervisory Authority shall be the supervisory authority of the EU/EEA Member State in which Hollowell's EU representative (if any) is established, or, absent such representative, the Irish Data Protection Commission (An Coimisiún um Chosaint Sonraí).
(e) Clause 17 (Governing law). Option 1 shall apply. The Standard Contractual Clauses shall be governed by the law of Ireland.
(f) Clause 18(b) (Choice of forum and jurisdiction). Any dispute arising from the Standard Contractual Clauses shall be resolved before the courts of Ireland.
12.2.2 The Annexes to the Standard Contractual Clauses shall be completed as follows:
(a) Annex I (List of Parties, Description of Transfer, Competent Supervisory Authority): Completed in accordance with the details set forth in Annex A to this DPA and the party information and Processing descriptions set forth in Sections 1, 4, and 5 of this DPA.
(b) Annex II (Technical and Organisational Measures): Completed by cross-reference to Section 7 of this DPA and Verdantia's SOC 2 Type II report audited by Clairemont & Sadler CPAs LLP.
(c) Annex III (List of Sub-processors): Completed by cross-reference to Appendix 1 to this DPA.
Section 12.3 — Transfer Impact Assessment
Verdantia shall cooperate with Hollowell in conducting any transfer impact assessment that may be required under Applicable Data Protection Laws, or by the guidance of competent Supervisory Authorities, prior to or in connection with the implementation of any cross-border transfer mechanism described in this Section 12. Such cooperation shall include providing information regarding Verdantia's technical and organizational measures, the legal regime applicable in the country of import, and any other information reasonably necessary for Hollowell to assess the level of data protection afforded to Personal Data following the transfer.
Section 13 — Audit Rights
Section 13.1 — Audit Scope and Frequency
13.1.1 Verdantia shall make available to Hollowell all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate Verdantia's compliance with its obligations under this DPA and Applicable Data Protection Laws, and shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by Hollowell or an independent third-party auditor mandated by Hollowell, in accordance with the procedures set forth in this Section 13.
13.1.2 Hollowell shall be entitled to conduct no more than one (1) audit per twelve-month period under this Section 13. Notwithstanding the foregoing, if a Data Breach has occurred affecting Personal Data Processed under this DPA, or if Hollowell has reasonable, documented grounds to suspect non-compliance by Verdantia with its obligations under this DPA, Hollowell may conduct one (1) additional audit in connection with such event during the relevant twelve-month period.
Section 13.2 — Notice and Scheduling
13.2.1 Hollowell shall provide Verdantia with at least twenty (20) business days' prior written notice of any audit to be conducted under this Section 13. Such notice shall specify the proposed scope and objectives of the audit, the anticipated duration of the audit, and the identity of any third-party auditor retained by Hollowell to conduct the audit. Any third-party auditor shall be bound by confidentiality obligations reasonably acceptable to Verdantia and shall not be a direct competitor of Verdantia in the markets in which Verdantia operates.
13.2.2 Audits shall be conducted during Verdantia's normal business hours and shall be planned and executed in a manner designed to minimize disruption to Verdantia's operations. Verdantia shall designate a point of contact to coordinate with Hollowell or its auditor and shall provide reasonable access to Verdantia's relevant facilities, systems, personnel, and records.
Section 13.3 — Costs
Each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with any audit conducted under this Section 13, including internal personnel costs, travel expenses, and fees of third-party auditors.
Section 13.4 — SOC 2 Report as Audit Substitute

Section 15 — Data Retention and Deletion
Section 15.1 — Retention During the Term
Verdantia shall retain Personal Data only for as long as is reasonably necessary to fulfill the Processing purposes described in Section 5 of this DPA and shall not retain Personal Data longer than is required under the Agreement, this DPA, or Applicable Data Protection Laws. Verdantia shall implement data retention policies and procedures consistent with this requirement and shall, upon Hollowell's reasonable request, provide information regarding the retention periods applicable to the categories of Personal Data Processed under this DPA.
Section 15.2 — Post-Termination Return and Deletion
15.2.1 Upon expiration or termination of the Agreement for any reason, Verdantia shall, at Hollowell's election (to be communicated to Verdantia in writing within fifteen (15) days of the effective date of such expiration or termination):
(a) Return all Personal Data to Hollowell in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (such as CSV, JSON, or XML), together with any decryption keys or other information necessary for Hollowell to access and use such Personal Data; or
(b) Securely delete all Personal Data in Verdantia's possession, custody, and control, using methods that render the Personal Data unrecoverable by commercially available means (including, without limitation, cryptographic erasure or physical destruction of storage media, as appropriate).
15.2.2 Verdantia shall complete such return or deletion within thirty (30) days of receiving Hollowell's written instruction. If Hollowell does not communicate its election within the fifteen (15) day period specified in Section 15.2.1, Verdantia shall securely delete all Personal Data in its possession, custody, and control within thirty (30) days following the effective date of expiration or termination of the Agreement.
15.2.3 Upon completion of the deletion of Personal Data, Verdantia shall provide written certification to Hollowell, signed by an authorized representative of Verdantia, confirming that all Personal Data has been securely deleted in accordance with this Section 15.2.
15.2.4 Notwithstanding the foregoing provisions of this Section 15.2, Verdantia may retain copies of Personal Data to the extent, and only to the extent, that such retention is required by applicable law or regulation (including, without limitation, tax, accounting, or regulatory record-keeping requirements), provided that: (i) such retained Personal Data shall remain subject to all applicable provisions of this DPA, including the confidentiality and security obligations set forth herein; (ii) Verdantia shall promptly inform Hollowell in writing of the specific legal or regulatory requirement necessitating such retention, identifying the applicable law or regulation and the expected duration of the retention period; and (iii) Verdantia shall securely delete such retained Personal Data promptly upon the expiration of the applicable legal retention obligation, and shall provide written certification of such deletion to Hollowell.
